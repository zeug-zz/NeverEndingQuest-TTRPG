# The Sunken Cartographer

## Introduction

An old cartographer's map leads the party beneath the flooded market to a
buried archive that no living soul has opened in a century. The adventure
premise: recover the last chart of the drowned city before the tide claims
it forever.

### Module Overview

The module presents a two-area descent into the Sunken Archive with a
central puzzle about the cartographer's compass and a final confrontation
with the tide warden.

## Introduction

### Running the Adventure

The party begins at the flooded market stair and may investigate the archive
entrance, the warden shrine, or the drowned ledger room in any order.

# Plot Overview

## PP001 -- The Drowned Invitation

A sealed map case arrives with the cartographer's mark. The party learns the
archive was sealed after the great flood.

## PP002 -- The Compass Chamber

The party aligns the compass with the three drowned stars to open the vault.

## PP003 -- The Tide Warden

The party confronts the warden and claims the last chart.

# Locations
