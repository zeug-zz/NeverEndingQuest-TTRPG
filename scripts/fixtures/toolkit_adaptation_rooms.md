# The Bell Tower Road

A short adventure about a bell tower whose warning chime has stopped.

## Room 1: The Gatehouse

The party finds a frightened keeper and a trail of wet footprints.

## Room 2: The Belfry Stairs

An unstable stair hides a choice between a risky climb and a safer detour.

## Room 3: The Bell Chamber

The final confrontation begins beside the silent bell.
