# Design

## Context

See `proposal.md` for motivation and `specs/tt-mise-en-scene-authoring-projection/spec.md` for the Study 3 contract. The existing Study 2 tooling separates request construction, delta validation, hydration, transport, materialization, and ledger checks; its tests include offline request building, fake transport, and fail-closed safety cases. Study 3 must be additive and isolated: no edits to Study 2 or its frozen protocol, code, tests, runtime/evidence, or verdict.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- Make each output-facing Study 3 validator rule auditable against an exact rendered request/schema rule or a canonical-input fact, while keeping host safety/integrity checks distinct.
- Verify references provider-free through the exact future request builder, fake transport, in-memory hydration, and the complete unchanged downstream safety-gate chain.
- Add collect-all diagnostics over untouched Study 2 delta copies, preserving the frozen study evidence and distinguishing violations from dependency-blocked `NOT_EVALUATED` checks.
- Keep identity, scene order, model envelope, cost inputs, and preparation/approval boundary fixed and inspectable.
- Correct compatibility of the existing production-boundary harness for this exact approved change without broadening its policy or allowing production paths.

**Non-Goals:**
- Repairing, migrating, or re-evaluating Study 1 or Study 2; changing the parent change or main specs; changing production runtime; changing any existing test/script except the single boundary-harness exception in Decision 9; or creating another capability.
- Provider/model calls, credentials, real attempt reservation or ledger initialization, generation, owner ratings, human semantic review, capability adjudication, or PR merge. Git publication to PR127 or a successor DRAFT PR is authorized by the current ruling, but is not performed by this planning-only update.

## Decisions

1. **Use an isolated Study 3 seam.** Add Study 3 protocol, request, contract, hydration adapter, sizing, collect-all diagnostic mode, and provider-free test artifacts only under a distinct `llm_staging_study3` namespace. Keep Study 2 byte-for-byte unchanged, including its original failures, values, prose, and frozen evidence. Run diagnostics only on copies outside the frozen evidence tree, with scratch under project-relative `tmp/router-rollout/mapping-pilot/mise-en-scene-offline/llm-staging-study3-prep/`; do not repair or write back to the copies. Do not reuse Study 2's output-facing contract as the Study 3 validator: that would preserve its namespace mismatch. Reuse only unchanged downstream host safety gates when the Study 3 hydrated shape satisfies their existing interfaces. The simplest alternative, editing Study 2 in place, is rejected because it would invalidate the frozen failure evidence.

2. **Represent parity as an auditable rule map plus semantic mutation and collect-all coverage.** Give each output-facing rule a stable identifier, category, validator check, and source declaration in the rendered prompt, injected schema, or exact canonical binding. Include IDs/namespaces, roles and stage order/uniqueness, allowed predicates and values, exact boolean `true` for the two stage relations, reference scope, public claim/span binding, agency fields, limits, unknown keys, stale hashes, overwrite attempts, and protected unknowns. Classify path, approval, ledger, credential, and transport protections as host safety/integrity, not model instructions. Tests must fail for both an unmapped new validator restriction and removal of a declared request/schema rule; semantic mutations and rule-to-check associations are required, not token-presence checks alone. The collect-all diagnostic reports every independently evaluable Study 2-copy violation and maps it to a declaration or host-integrity rule. A check whose prerequisite is invalid or unavailable is `NOT_EVALUATED` with its dependency, never a false pass or crash. The rule inventory, bidirectional mutations, and three-example diagnostic coverage are complementary evidence, not a proof that no conceivable hidden rule remains or that natural-language completeness is formally proven.

3. **Keep the delta-only boundary and references deterministic.** Bind each hand-assembled reference to its exact frozen expected-input and source-material IDs/hashes and approved topology. Accept only declared references and authored delta fields; reject unknown keys, non-finite values, duplicate keys, stale/unknown hashes, disallowed references, and base/topology overwrite attempts before hydration. Keep source/base IDs distinct from module-qualified `<module_id>:fact:study3-authored:<suffix>` IDs. Use one hand-assembled reference per scene, never generated text or an accessed/rated model output. The Study 2 diagnostic reads only byte-preserving copies and does not rewrite their values or prose.

4. **Exercise the actual future request path without transport.** The fake transport receives the exact request object produced by the Study 3 request builder and records only allowlisted request-shape data needed for provider-free assertions. Tripwires prohibit HTTP, credentials, external provider construction, and real ledger access. The builder's ordered bindings are exactly `into_the_deepvault:scene:ha003-i02`, `into_the_deepvault:scene:sd005-i03`, and `xhalruuns_masquerade:scene:tsu001-ao02`. The approved envelope is OpenRouter `openai/gpt-6-luna`, `allow_fallbacks=false`, no retries/reruns/tools/redirects/streaming/continuation, 16,384 max tokens, and a 720-second timeout. The cap approval is not authorization to dispatch; do not add a live Study 3 client or transport in this preparation scope.

5. **Hydrate in memory, then run all existing downstream gates.** The Study 3 delta validator and hydration adapter own the new namespace and output rules. Hydration must not mutate or persist canonical inputs. Run every applicable unchanged downstream safety gate on the complete hydrated fixture and assert the gate inventory is exhausted; schema acceptance alone is not passage. Keep host safety results separately labeled from output-contract results.

6. **Make sizing offline, bounded, and auditable.** Derive per-scene request/output sizing from the hand-assembled references and the already hash-bound historical rate record. Report approximately USD 0.01 expected and approximately USD 0.03 conservative for three 16,384-token output envelopes as estimates; the separate hard ceiling is USD 0.05. Any future host preflight must conservatively price the full three-call envelope strictly below USD 0.05 or refuse before dispatch. Missing, stale, or invalid rate provenance blocks priced reporting; do not repeat the availability GET or substitute an unverified rate. Future actual accounting that is missing, pending, or inconsistent stops safely.

7. **Pin cap approval without self-asserted state; keep readiness and dispatch separate.** The approved three-attempt cap belongs to fixed identity `mise-en-scene-study3-20261110`, not a run ID or run root: exactly three attempts, one per approved scene, OpenRouter `openai/gpt-6-luna`, no fallback or retry, 16,384 max tokens, 720-second timeout, approximately USD 0.01 expected, approximately USD 0.03 conservative, and a hard USD 0.05 ceiling. Its owner approval is supplied by the authorized Overseer under `neq-standing-authority-20261110` and pinned by `neq-study3-pre-approved-20261110`; do not require another owner-only approval or accept a mutable self-asserted approval JSON as authority. Provider-free synthetic preflight tests must verify the exact ruling pins and envelope, and refuse missing/incorrect authorization or unsafe states before reservation, credentials, and HTTP. Preparation uses no real ledger or allocation. The cap does not establish software readiness or authorize calls; dispatch remains blocked until the review packet receives Overseer software-preparation review and the Overseer explicitly records READY and authorizes execution.

8. **Separate software preparation from study outcomes.** A deterministic reference pass alone leaves software-preparation readiness PENDING. It may become READY only after bidirectional parity, collect-all diagnostic coverage and mapped findings, all three exact-request/full-gate reference checks, focused Study 3 regression, the 196-test full mise-en-scene baseline suite, the 276-test mapping suite, complete unchanged frozen-file inventories/hashes, all 11 required CI checks on the exact new head of DRAFT PR127 or a successor DRAFT PR, and explicit Overseer software-preparation review pass. A green old head is not evidence for the new head. Git publication is authorized by `neq-study3-apply-20261110`; never merge. After the packet and exact-head checks are ready, stop for Overseer software-preparation review before actual calls. Software readiness is independent of semantic review, owner ratings, and model-capability verdict, which may remain PENDING. No generation, allocation, or provider execution is part of this preparation or review gate.

9. **Keep the boundary-harness correction exact and fail-closed.** The only authorized edit to an existing test/script is `scripts/test_mapping_pilot_boundaries.py`: append exactly `openspec/changes/v2-mise-en-scene-study3-contract-parity/` to its current production-boundary allowlist, then add focused positive/negative assertions showing that a path under this exact prefix is admitted, an adjacent similarly prefixed sibling is rejected, and production paths remain rejected. Preserve all other allowlist entries and production-forbidden checks. Do not add a generic `openspec/changes/` prefix, skip or disable tests, or edit any other test/script or production/runtime path. This repairs compatibility with the already-approved change tree; it does not authorize arbitrary OpenSpec changes or alter production-boundary policy. The currently reported mapping failure is pending this fix and rerun, not a completed gate.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **[Rule-map drift can become self-confirming]** -> Keep independent negative mutations for validator additions and request/schema removals, and require each rule-map entry to point to a semantic assertion rather than only matching strings.
- **[Study 3 adapter may not match a downstream gate's current input shape]** -> Verify the interface read-only during implementation; use a narrow in-memory adapter and do not edit or weaken the unchanged gate. Treat an unadaptable or failed gate as blocked.
- **[Sizing estimates may be mistaken for a price guarantee]** -> Label expected and conservative figures as estimates, preserve the historical rate hash, keep the separate USD 0.05 hard ceiling, and require any future conservative preflight total to be strictly below it.
- **[Passing hand-built references or three diagnostic examples may be mistaken for model evidence or exhaustive rule proof]** -> Label references deterministic software fixtures and diagnostics `DIAGNOSTIC_NOT_STUDY_RESULT`; combine the rule inventory, bidirectional semantic mutations, and collect-all coverage without claiming that three examples prove there are no other hidden rules. Require every R19 software gate and Overseer review before software readiness is READY, while keeping semantic review, owner ratings, and capability verdict separate and PENDING.
- **[Cost estimates or mutable approval state may be mistaken for dispatch authority]** -> Preserve the distinct USD 0.05 hard ceiling, require a conservative future preflight strictly below it, pin approvals to the exact ruling IDs and envelope, and wait for Overseer software-preparation review plus explicit call authorization.

## Migration Plan

No runtime or persisted-data migration is required. The apply workflow adds only the isolated Study 3 preparation seam, provider-free checks, and diagnostics on copies. It must not rewrite or re-materialize Study 1/2 artifacts. Commit/push to PR127 or a successor DRAFT PR is authorized; exact-new-head CI must pass all 11 checks, then the packet stops for Overseer software-preparation review. Never merge. There is no dispatch enablement or real ledger setup in this change; no rollback of existing study data is needed.

## Open Questions

None. The cap is approved under the pinned standing-authority and pre-approval rulings. Software-preparation review and dispatch authorization remain pending and are separate gates.
