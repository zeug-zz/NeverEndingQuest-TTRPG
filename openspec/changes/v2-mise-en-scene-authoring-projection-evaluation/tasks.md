All tasks are prospective and intentionally unchecked. They describe only offline scene-relationship authoring/projection prototypes and their separately gated evaluation. Proposed code/test paths do not yet exist. This change authorizes no implementation, runtime/schema/prompt changes, module writes/backfill, provider calls, live listener, or capture.

If evaluation needs runtime or prompt/injection integration, its independent owning runtime/injection contract must also approve that integration. None is current budget or permission; offline prototype approval cannot authorize integration.
