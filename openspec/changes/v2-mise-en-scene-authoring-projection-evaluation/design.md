## Goals and non-goals

**Goals**

- Prototype a compact scene relationship artifact and deterministic projections that capture an actionable stage rather than merely a valid schema or feature list.
- Preserve extraction unknowns while allowing clearly marked Builder-authored staging inside approved topology.
- Test scene scope/orientation, approach/threshold/focal relations, multi-cell extent, usable space, fixed/mobile identity, frontage identity, and guarded knowledge with provider-free fixtures.
- Compare matched facts in four conditions: structured relationships (A), prose-only control (B), structured relationships plus schematic grid (C), and those structured relationships plus grid plus Builder-authored staged arrival prose (D). D presents the same facts, not extra facts; no production format is selected.
- Preregister a small, new, leakage-checked evaluation only after the owner separately approves its manifest, rubric, thresholds, provider, and budget.
- State that supplied-fixture validation/projection does not demonstrate that an LLM Builder can stage scenes. Any later LLM-authored staging evaluation requires separate authorization, its own unapproved budget/protocol/source provenance, and is not a current task or provider call.

**Non-goals**

- Production S1-S5 or SC0-SC4 implementation, tactical AI, full movement enforcement, combat maps, Network clients/transport, prompt integration, persistence, canonical module/schema changes, or existing-module backfill decisions.
- Acceptance of ADR-0034, a production TITAN seed job, authority transfer to TITAN, or Firelight migration of any rule family.
- Re-running, editing, pooling, or rescoring M1/SP3/SP3b evidence; treating owner examples as held-out; or using any proposed evaluation number as approved/frozen. D1 and transcript-owner judgments remain pending, and there is no final format/combat/data choice.
- Production core/web/modules/schema/prompts, ADR-0034 acceptance, SP3b reopening, frozen artifacts/ratings changes, TITAN seed generation, and live/provider evaluation remain unauthorized. Runtime Narrator behavior is not implemented by this prototype.
- Exhaustive atmospheric modeling. The prototype should encode only enough relationships to make the staged scene understandable and actionable.

## Prototype boundary and design choices

The future prototype seam is deliberately offline: supplied source facts and approved topology enter as immutable fixture inputs; an authoring/projection function emits relationship artifacts and diagnostics; tests compare input, authored facts, and render outputs. This validates/projects supplied fixtures only; it is not an LLM Builder test and does not demonstrate that an LLM can stage scenes. Any future LLM-authored staging study needs separate authorization, source provenance, and its own unapproved protocol and budget, outside both proposed evaluation ledgers. There is no module write, runtime-state mutation, provider call, live listener, campaign migration, runtime Narrator, or production consumer.

1. **Provenance and creativity are separate.** Extracted claims cite consulted source material and leave unsupported relationships unknown. A Builder prototype may add useful initial scene staging as new authorship, but may not contradict supplied topology or relabel new decisions as source facts. Runtime PCs from prior play are not initial-scene content.
2. **Scene staging is relational, not decorative.** Each fixture states its scope and distinguishes extraction unknowns from explicitly Builder-authored orientation. Extraction without compass evidence remains unknown and never defaults north; a Builder may author an orientation such as north-up only when marked as authored and checked against approved topology/compass frames. Fixtures record applicable approaches, thresholds, focal relationships, path/terrain/boundary extents, and usable space. A schema-valid feature inventory with missing required positional relationships fails coverage diagnostics.
3. **Cells do not imply rules.** Cell positions are schematic. They do not establish compass direction, distance, scale, access, doors, line of sight, audibility, travel time, movement cost, or legality. An unknown remains unknown unless a separate source or explicitly marked, validated Builder decision establishes it; schematic placement never supplies metrics or legality.
4. **Identity survives multiple views.** Fixed anchors and mobile occupants are separate. One tavern has one shared identity across linked scene views while its different frontages remain separate; neither frontage nor proximity creates a door or exit.
5. **Knowledge is audience-scoped in the fixture.** The GM-aware camp fixture can contain its guard condition. Public arrival and map output cannot expose or activate the surprise from arrival, looking, or tent investigation. Attempted fire-lighting is the sole trigger condition; its effect is unknown, not a license to invent a result.
6. **The projection arms are matched evidence, not an architecture vote.** A is structured relationships; B is the prose-only matched-facts control; C adds a schematic grid to A; D adds Builder-authored staged arrival prose to C. All four bind to the same normalized semantic facts, topology, fact IDs, and provenance, including any sensory-gradient or affordance facts used. D adds presentation/staging, not factual information. Report A-versus-C as the descriptive grid increment, C-versus-D as the descriptive staging increment, and D-versus-B as the combined-shape comparison; do not claim causality, an automatic winner, or a production-format decision.
7. **Arrival prose is fact-bound staging.** Builder-authored prose's approach-to-threshold-to-focal hierarchy, authored/known light/sound/weather gradients, topology/perception-valid transition toward the next section, and visible invitations to act must be traceable to structured facts and provenance. Unknown/non-applicable cues stay so. Checkable prose-only facts, topology/anchor changes, fabricated gradients, guarded leaks, and prescribed choices/outcomes fail diagnostics; non-actionable atmospheric framing may remain flexible.

### Assertion and rating rules

- Freeze each assertion before evaluation and classify it as critical, non-critical, required-but-unknown, or non-applicable. Count an assertion once per declared episode/turn, not once per repeated narration mention.
- **Critical zero-tolerance failures:** public leakage or false activation of the guarded camp surprise; a protected unknown stated as known (including fabricated trigger effect, route, compass direction, sensory gradient, metric, or mechanics); topology/route contradiction; prose relocating an anchor or adding a checkable actionable fact absent from structured facts; movement by narration without an authorized commit in a future runtime gate; runtime-PC leakage into initial authorship; or a door/access/legality inference from a schematic relationship. Any critical violation fails its condition/party-shape cell regardless of averages.
- **Non-critical denominator:** for each condition and party-shape cell, `n` is the number of predeclared non-critical assertions with enough evidence to adjudicate as correct/acceptable or incorrect. Incorrect assertions enter the denominator only and never the correct/acceptable numerator. Candidate threshold: at least 90% correct/acceptable in every cell. A single non-critical miss is not an automatic gate failure, but it remains in the denominator and may cause the cell to miss the rate threshold.
- A required assertion with missing artifact, turn, state, or rating evidence makes the relevant gate BLOCKED; it is not counted as success or included as a favorable denominator item. A required protected-unknown assertion is evaluated as “must remain unknown”; making a concrete claim is critical. A non-required unknown or genuinely non-applicable outcome is excluded with a reason, never counted as a pass.
- Candidate owner threshold: mean legibility and usefulness each at least 4.0/5 **in each condition separately**, alongside artifact-level scores and ranges. No pooling across conditions or P1/P4. Candidate rates and ratings are not ratified and do not revise M1's frozen 3.5 threshold or result.
- Report A-versus-C as the descriptive grid increment, C-versus-D as the descriptive staging increment, and D-versus-B as the combined-shape result. Do not pool conditions, party shapes, development examples, or holdout scenes, hide a failing cell, claim general causality, or infer an automatic winner or production-format choice.

### Provider-free diagnostics and future live ownership

Required negative fixture pairs distinguish (1) a fact absent from source and correctly left unknown, (2) a source-supported fact omitted during extraction/authoring, and (3) an authored fact lost by a projection or renderer. A positional-coverage report must identify the missing relationship/extent and the stage at which it disappeared; schema validity alone is insufficient. Include negative fixtures for wrong/defaulted compass and conflicting Builder orientation, missing approach/threshold/focal relations, truncated multi-cell path/terrain/boundary, no usable space, inferred tavern door, changed fixed anchor, PC contamination, public camp leak/false trigger, fabricated metric/mechanics or light/sound/weather gradient, prose-only actionable facts, and prescriptive choices/success. All checks are deterministic and provider-free.

If live evaluation is separately approved, the primary orchestrator—not a builder—owns the isolated Capture_Arena run and final gate verdict after focused tests. Any required runtime or injection integration must first be approved by its independent owning contract/team. Follow `neq-capture-arena`: use a real copy under project-relative `tmp/combat_capture/`, port 8360, `NEQ_OPEN_BROWSER=0`, and the repository venv. Preserve per-gate/per-run evidence under `tmp/router-rollout/mise-en-scene/<gate>/<run-id>/`, including frozen manifest and assignment seed/order, artifact hashes, scene/party/condition identity, worktree and model/provider identity, exact expected/observed assertions, transcripts, before/after state where applicable, ratings, and artifact paths. Do not run a provider, start a server, or create capture evidence under this planning-only change.
