# Proposal

## Why

The frozen M1 packet averaged 2.625/5 for legibility and 2.875/5 for usefulness, below its frozen 3.5 thresholds. The owner review identifies actionable mise-en-scene, not grid decoration, as the goal. This prospective follow-up isolates an offline scene-relationship authoring/projection prototype and a leakage-controlled evaluation protocol; it does not convert pilot evidence or redesign examples into results.

## Central objective: Narrator-first

The prototype optimizes the NEQ LLM's spatial comprehension and actionable, coherent narration first. Cumulative knowledge-filtered player maps are secondary projections of the same authored facts plus committed discoveries—not a full atlas or production S5.

## Scope

- Define testable offline requirements for provenance, unknowns, scene scope/orientation, approaches and thresholds, focal relationships, multi-cell path/terrain/boundary extents, usable space, and stable fixed/mobile anchors. Preserve Builder creativity inside approved topology; do not infer doors, routes, compass bearings, metrics, or mechanics.
- Compare matched semantic facts as structured relationships, equivalent prose-only, and the same structured facts plus a schematic grid. This is an evaluation comparison, not a production-format selection.
- Specify provider-free structural and positional-coverage diagnostics, then a proposed development/calibration and new held-out evaluation protocol. Proposed counts, thresholds, attempts, request ceilings, and live time are for owner review only; they are not an approved budget or authorization.
- Keep production S/SC integration, runtime prompt/schema/state changes, existing-module backfill, Network client work, TITAN seed generation, and Firelight family migration in their separately reviewed owner changes.
- This change revises only planning artifacts in this change directory. It does not accept ADR-0034, require its acceptance for offline prototypes, alter main specs or pilot records, call a provider, or start a live gate.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `tt-mise-en-scene-authoring-projection`: A prospective contract for offline scene-staging/relationship prototypes, matched projections, provider-free diagnostics, and separately authorized evaluation; it does not specify production runtime integration.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing spatial, scene-authority, Builder, and narrator specs continue to own their current bounded contracts; this capability defines their proposed cross-track seam without silently replacing them.

## Impact

Future prototype code and tests, if separately approved, would use proposed paths under `scripts/mise_en_scene/` and `scripts/test_mise_en_scene_*.py`; those paths are not claimed to exist. Evaluation scratch artifacts would remain under project-relative `tmp/router-rollout/mapping-pilot/mise-en-scene-planning/` (and separately authorized live captures under `tmp/router-rollout/mise-en-scene/<gate>/<run-id>/`). No production schema, module, runtime, prompt, persistence, or provider path is in scope here. Network retains authorization, transport, and client ownership. Under accepted ADR-0033, Firelight decides legality only for migrated families through the Python bridge; unmigrated families retain Python guards. ADR-0034 remains Proposed; a runtime owner change needs an accepted or revised authority decision, but offline prototypes do not depend on accepting that draft.
