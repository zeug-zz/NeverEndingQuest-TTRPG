# A Pottsfield Burial

## Introduction

### Module Overview
In this eerie harvest-season adventure, the party arrives at Pottsfield beneath a banner of golden grain and smiling lantern-light, only to find the town fraying at the edges under the weight of whispered disappearances, restless dead, and a bargain gone sour long ago. What begins as a simple inquiry soon unfurls into a mist-wreathed mystery of masked revels, haunted parish grounds, shadowed woods, and ancient crypts, all threaded together by secrets the townsfolk would rather keep buried than harvest. Within these pages lie 11 memorable NPCs, 7 interlocking plot points, 6 evocative locations, and 13 creature stat blocks to populate the road, the grave, and the dark places beneath the fields.

### The Story So Far
The party comes to Pottsfield at the height of the Harvest Festival, where the town's painted cheer cannot quite conceal the tremor beneath it. At the outskirts, anxious villagers speak of missing kin and cold shapes moving between the wheat. In the Hearthlands, a masked masquerade turns merriment into misdirection, and every smile seems to hide a bargain. The trail leads onward into the Parishlands, where graves are disturbed, voices rise from the earth at night, and the dead seem unwilling to remain silent. Bit by bit, the party learns that Pottsfield's prosperity was bought with a pact older than the festival itself, and that something in the town's history has begun to claw its way back into the light.

From there, the road bends through the Shrouded Woods, where fog coils around the trunks like pale fingers and spectral warnings hang in the air. Hidden paths and half-truths draw the party toward the Veilwood Expanse and the buried ways beneath the Harvest Grounds, where the Shrouded Necropolis waits in vine-choked darkness. There, the truth of the curse gathers shape: a bound spirit's influence, forbidden rites, and the corruption of Gloamroot feeding the land's misery. The final trail leads to the Reaping Fields, where the curse's heart beats beneath a crimson sky and the Shadow Harvester stands ready to claim what remains of Pottsfield. If the party prevails, the mists begin to lift and the dead may finally rest; if they fail, the town is left to rot in a new and terrible season of haunting.

### Running the Adventure
This adventure is designed for a party of 3rd--5th level characters and works best with a group of four to five adventurers, though it can be adjusted for smaller or larger parties by tuning combat difficulty and the weight of social scenes. Before play, the Dungeon Master should familiarize themselves with the town's key NPCs, the shifting loyalties of the villagers, and the way each location reveals another layer of the curse, since the story relies on clues, atmosphere, and player choices as much as on battle. A little preparation for haunted ambience, festival intrigue, and the final confrontation will help Pottsfield bloom into a memorable place of dread and wonder.

# Plot Overview

When the party reaches the golden outskirts of Pottsfield, they find a village dressed in harvest finery and rotting from within: lanterns glow, masks smile, and every cheerful song seems to hide a whispered name of the missing. Deputy Silas Cragg begs for help, but the truth lies deeper than any one disappearance--beneath the parish graves, through the mist-choked woods, and into ancient crypts where a forgotten pact still binds the dead to the living. As ghostly warnings grow bolder and the harvest revels turn uncanny, the party must untangle a web of bargains, buried sins, and a creeping corruption known only in fearful fragments as Gloamroot. At the heart of the cursed fields, where crimson skies stain the earth and the Shadow Harvester waits to reap what was promised, they must decide whether Pottsfield is to be saved, damned, or remade by the secrets it has fed the dark.

### PP001 -- Shadows Over Pottsfield: Arrival at the Outskirts

The party arrives at the edge of Pottsfield (PO001), greeted by golden fields and the anxious preparations for the Harvest Festival. Despite the cheerful facade, rumors of missing villagers and restless spirits abound. Deputy Silas Cragg requests their help investigating disappearances, while suggestions point toward the town center and the old parish grounds.

### PP002 -- Masks and Misdirection: The Hearthlands' Revels

Following leads from the outskirts, the party heads into Pottsfield Hearthlands (PVC001), where a masked harvest masquerade is stirring unease amid forced cheer. The enigmatic Masked Reveler and Widow Elsie Tallow hold secrets about the town's pacts and the Parish Grounds. Social intrigue and careful questioning are vital, as villagers' attitudes depend on the party's tact.

### PP003 -- The Unquiet Parish: Investigating the Parishlands

The party moves to Pottsfield Parishlands (PPG001), where desperate townsfolk report ghostly apparitions, unearthed graves, and chilling voices at night. Bound spirits hint at an ancient pact sealed in the crypts beneath the Harvest Grounds. The party must navigate between the living and the dead, uncovering the truth behind Pottsfield's bargains and the origins of the curse.

### PP004 -- Through the Shrouded Woods to Veilwood Expanse

Spectral clues and omens direct the party to the Shrouded Woods (TSW001), a haunted wilderness separating Pottsfield from the deeper crypts. Here, they encounter the Specter of Jonny Harrow and Father Milgrom, both offering cryptic warnings or bargains. The woods' mists hint at a greater power--Gloamroot--whose corruption may be fueling Pottsfield's curse. The party discovers a hidden entrance to the Ancient Crypts and may choose to explore the Veilwood's heart.

### PP005 -- Secrets Beneath: The Shrouded Necropolis

The party descends into the vine-choked tombs of the Shrouded Necropolis (TAC001), beneath the Harvest Grounds. Here, ancient graves have been disturbed and forbidden rituals echo in the gloom. The party must navigate shifting shadows, deathly traps, and confront the Bound Spirit's growing influence. The choices made thus far--regarding spirits, relics, and townsfolk--determine the necropolis' dangers and the outcome of the final ritual.

### PP006 -- The Reaping Fields: Curse's Heart

To ensure the curse is truly broken, the party must journey to the infamous Reaping Fields (THG001), where vanishing travelers, crimson skies, and the Shadow Harvester threaten Pottsfield's future. Here, the party must use all they've learned to uncover the origin of the curse, disrupt the final ritual, and face the Shadow Harvester in a climactic confrontation. The fate of Pottsfield and its restless dead hangs in the balance.

### PP007 -- Breaking the Veil: Consequences and Dawn

With the ritual disrupted and the curse's heart confronted, the mists begin to recede across Pottsfield, the Parishlands, and the haunted fields. Freed spirits guide the party back through the Veilwood Expanse. The survivors cautiously emerge, and the fate of Pottsfield--salvation, spectral havoc, or a new cycle--rests on the party's choices. The adventure closes with the region forever changed and the party's legacy echoing in Pottsfield's fields.

# Locations

## PO001 -- Fields of Pottsfield

Golden fields ripple around Pottsfield like a living tapestry, their wheat and pumpkins glowing under a broad, kind sky. The town feels almost impossibly wholesome: tidy cottages lean close together, woodsmoke curls from neat chimneys, and every passing villager offers a smile that seems practiced by generations of good harvests. Yet the place has a peculiar stillness beneath its cheer, a hush that settles over the roads and hedgerows as if the land itself is holding its breath. That contrast gives Pottsfield its distinctive charm--an inviting pastoral haven with just enough unease to make a Dungeon Master hint that something is not quite right beneath the laughter and larks.

The town's key spaces support the opening beats of the adventure by drawing newcomers in through ordinary comforts before the shadows begin to lengthen. The market near the outskirts serves as the first point of contact with Pottsfield's daily life, where Martha Finch greets travelers with practical warmth and a keen eye for anything unusual among the produce and gossip. The guildhall, with Ezra Pike and its funereal neatness, quietly suggests that Pottsfield's relationship with death is more formal than most villages would admit, while the Stillroot Tavern, under Old Nan Berryman's watchful hospitality, becomes the social heart where rumors, local history, and uneasy omens can surface. Together these places funnel the party toward the parishlands beyond town, linking the safe, familiar center of Pottsfield to the darker truths waiting in the fields and the next stage of the plot.

Pottsfield itself is lightly defended by common folk rather than warriors, so the true threats here are more atmospheric than martial: suspicious strangers, whispered superstitions, and the possibility that the town's calm has been carefully maintained. The NPCs are friendly on the surface, but each carries a different piece of the local mood--Martha's bustling practicality, Ezra's grave restraint, and Old Nan's knowing, fireside authority. From here, the adventure naturally extends outward into the Pottsfield Parishlands, where the town's comforting facade gives way to broader mysteries and whatever shadows are gathering at the edges of the harvest.

**Area Type:** Town | **Recommended Level:** 3

### A01 -- Silent Harvest Market

Set near the entry road to Pottsfield Outskirts, this open-air market is lined with rustic wooden stalls beneath awnings of faded calico. Each stall boasts seasonal vegetables, gourds, root crops, and preserved goods. A peculiar hush hangs over the market--shoppers and vendors converse in soft tones, as if careful not to disturb something slumbering beneath the soil. The scent of fresh bread, apples, and dried herbs mingles pleasantly with the crisp autumn air.

*Danger Level: Low*

*Accessibility: Easy to access from the main road with wide lanes between stalls; no barriers for most creatures.*

>This is the town's social and economic center. Most villagers pass through here, and the market is a good place for the party to gather rumors, meet Martha Finch, and spot the first hints of Pottsfield's secret.

**Features:**

- **Display Tables**: Sturdy oak tables covered with burlap, topped with a variety of produce, jars, and trinkets. Some cloths are stitched with strange symbols or protective wards.
- **Old Well**: A covered stone well at the market's center, ringed by faded scarecrows. Its waters are said to be especially sweet, but locals never drink after dusk.

**DC Checks:**

- Insight DC 12: Notice villagers seem nervous or overly polite.
- Perception DC 13: Spot faint muddy footprints leading behind a vendor's tent.
- Persuasion DC 14: Convince Martha Finch to reveal more about the town's troubles.

**Plot Hooks:**

- A masked reveler drops a pumpkin-shaped coin purse before vanishing into the crowd.
- A nervous vendor insists they've seen something strange moving among the market stalls long after dusk.
- Martha Finch offers the party a free meal at Stillroot Tavern if they help her find a missing delivery.

**Loot:**

- Fresh harvest basket (worth 10 gp)
- Vial of sweet cider
- Bundle of dried medicinal herbs (use as healer's kit, 1d6 uses)
- Hand-carved wooden whistle

**NPCs:**

- Martha Finch (Welcoming, guarded)

*Connected to: Within area: A02, A03*

**DM Guidance:**

Silent Harvest Market is the party's first real introduction to Pottsfield's peculiar atmosphere. NPCs will not mention the curse or the embalmer's activities openly but can be persuaded with successful social skill checks (see DCs). Place Martha Finch here, who can introduce the main plot and redirect suspicious inquiries. Consider adding environmental clues: odd footprints, lingering spectral chill, or a stall unmanned but displaying goods.

### A02 -- Embalmer's Guildhall

Tucked between the market and a patch of crooked trees, this somber stone building is draped with heavy black bunting and carved motifs of harvest and repose. The interior is uncomfortably clean, its silence broken only by the ticking of a metronome in the embalming room. The air smells of incense, preserving oils, and faintly of decay. Members of the guild wear pumpkin-orange sashes over plain brown smocks. Outsiders are not welcome beyond the foyer without permission.

*Danger Level: Low*

*Accessibility: Generally accessible during daytime; guild members scrutinize all visitors. Night access requires stealth or a key.*

>Here, the party may uncover evidence of the town's unusual burial practices and find their first real hint at the necromantic forces at work. Ezra Pike can offer information or a quest if persuaded.

**Features:**

- **Interesting Architecture**: Stonework is inlaid with fossilized corn husks and carved gourds, incorporating funerary symbols unique to Pottsfield.
- **Preparation Table**: A large marble slab, meticulously clean, surrounded by racks of embalming tools and vials.

**DC Checks:**

- Investigation DC 13: Find the hidden ledger of burials.
- Stealth DC 14: Sneak into the embalming chamber undetected.
- Medicine DC 12: Identify traces of necromantic reagents.

**Plot Hooks:**

- Ezra Pike is quietly seeking help to investigate a recent disturbance in the crypts beneath the parish grounds.
- A masked Reveler is seen slipping out the back door, leaving muddy footprints.
- A town burial is scheduled for sunset; Ezra quietly warns the party not to attend unless they wish to see 'things better left unseen.'

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 14 | Disable DC 12 | Trigger DC 10 | Damage: Poison needle trap (1d8 poison damage, CON save DC 12 to resist)

**Doors:**

- Front Door -- Heavy oak reinforced with iron bands, bearing the guild's knotwork emblem.
- Back Door -- Narrow, utilitarian, leads to a small herb garden. -- Locked (DC 13) -- Key: Guildhall Key

**Loot:**

- Vial of rare embalming oil (10 gp)
- Set of bone carving tools (artisan's tools)
- Silvered sickle (weapon)
- Ledger of town burials (contains cryptic notes)

**NPCs:**

- Ezra Pike (Reserved, businesslike)

*Connected to: Within area: A01*

**DM Guidance:**

The guildhall is a good place for the party to investigate the town's handling of death. Ezra Pike will not discuss the necromantic curse unless pressured or unless the party presents evidence. The ledger may contain hints about missing villagers or prior burials gone awry. Allow for stealth or social infiltration if the party wishes to explore deeper.

### A03 -- The Stillroot Tavern

At the edge of the market, the Stillroot is a sprawling, timber-framed tavern with a thatched roof and a sign depicting a grinning pumpkin. Lanterns flicker from every beam, illuminating crowded tables, a roaring hearth, and a trophy wall displaying antique farming implements. The air here is lively with the scent of spiced ale and roasted squash. Locals gather to share stories and songs, but fall strangely quiet whenever certain topics arise.

*Danger Level: Low*

*Accessibility: Always open to travelers, but staff may refuse service to those causing trouble.*

>The Stillroot is the social heart of Pottsfield, perfect for rumor-gathering, games, and learning about the village's history. Old Nan can steer the party toward important plot threads.

**Features:**

- **Bar Counter**: Worn oak bar lined with mismatched stools and jars of pickled eggs and vegetables.
- **Hearth**: A massive stone fireplace provides warmth; above it hangs a faded tapestry depicting a harvest festival.
- **Trophy Display**: A wall adorned with antique farming tools and strange, whimsical masks from past festivals.

**DC Checks:**

- Insight DC 12: Notice the tavern staff growing anxious if the party asks about missing villagers.
- Perception DC 13: Find the hidden compartment in the trophy display.
- Performance DC 14: Win over the crowd and gain local trust.

**Plot Hooks:**

- Old Nan Berryman tells a tale about the 'Dancing Spirits of the Harvest,' hinting at the village's curse.
- A drunken masked reveler offers to show the party a secret if they win a game of dice.
- A wall trophy holds a hidden compartment containing a cryptic note.

**Doors:**

- Front Door -- Twin swinging doors with carved pumpkin motifs.
- Cellar Hatch -- Iron-bound wooden hatch behind the bar, leading to a root cellar. -- Locked (DC 12) -- Key: Tavern Cellar Key

**Loot:**

- Jug of spiced pumpkin ale
- Map to a hidden grove in the Shrouded Woods
- Carved bone dice (worth 5 gp to collectors)
- Tavern ledger with coded notes

**NPCs:**

- Old Nan Berryman (Welcoming, mischievous)

*Connected to: Within area: A01; The Pottsfield Parishlands / C01*

**DM Guidance:**

This is the best place for roleplay and rumor-gathering. Old Nan Berryman provides both comic relief and clues if befriended. If the party pries too hard into the town's secrets, the patrons grow suspicious. The map in the lootTable can be used to direct the party to The Shrouded Woods.

## PPG001 -- The Pottsfield Parishlands

The Pottsfield Parishlands lie under a shroud of mist and memory, where weathered gravestones lean from the soft earth like teeth in a half-buried jaw. Distant bells toll through the haze, their sound answered by the low murmur of unseen congregants and the creak of yew branches in a wind that never quite reaches the skin. The place feels wrong in a subtle, lingering way: the ground gives underfoot as though something stirs beneath the roots, and every field path seems to lead past another sign of old grief, old rites, and old refusals to rest. What makes the parishlands distinctive is not just their graveyard hush, but the sense that the living and the dead still share the same uneasy parish, each watching the other through fog and fencepost.

This area ties directly into the adventure's investigation of the Unquiet Parish, serving as one of the clearest places to uncover the truth behind the town's lingering spiritual unrest. The scattered holdings here are not merely atmospheric set dressing; they are clues in physical form, each one hinting at a failed rite, a broken burial custom, or a congregation that never truly dispersed. Desperate townsfolk drift through the crossroads seeking aid or absolution, while bound spirits and restless dead cling to the older places of remembrance, resisting any attempt to quiet them. Crawling claws and a restless skeleton make it plain that the dead are not at peace, and that something in the parishlands has been disturbed, trapped, or denied proper burial.

The parishlands connect naturally to the broader adventure by opening routes back toward the Fields of Pottsfield and onward into the Pottsfield Hearthlands, making them both a destination and a threshold. The crossroads serve as a grim meeting point where rumors, pleas, and omens can be gathered before the party pushes deeper into the town's troubles. From here, the DM can let the players feel the parishlands as a place of transition: a haunted margin between the cultivated safety of the hearthlands and the more open, vulnerable fields beyond. Every path through the mist suggests another answer waiting just out of sight, and every answer seems to lead either to the living town above or to the darker truths buried beneath it.

**Area Type:** Mixed | **Recommended Level:** 4

### C01 -- Sable Crossroads

Where three narrow, rutted paths merge amid a circle of leaning headstones. The crossroads is shrouded in perpetual dimness, the air thick with dust and the scent of moss. The tolling of distant bells seems to linger here, echoing between the stones. Deep wagon grooves and scattered bone fragments suggest this was once a place of frequent passage--and perhaps hasty burials.

*Danger Level: Medium*

*Accessibility: Easily reached by any of the main paths through the Parishlands.*

>The Sable Crossroads sets the tone for the haunted Parish Grounds, presenting a choice of paths and an immediate taste of the supernatural threat underlying Pottsfield's stillness.

**Features:**

- **Dust**: A thick, gray dust coats every surface, dulling colors and muffling sound. It tastes faintly of ash.
- **Converging Paths**: Three distinct, timeworn paths meet under a ring of leaning headstones, some chiseled with symbols of old faiths.
- **Recent Disturbance**: Fresh earth and a toppled grave marker hint at something recently unearthed.

**DC Checks:**

- Perception DC 13: Notice fresh disturbed earth and faint, unnatural scratching beneath a nearby stone.
- Insight DC 12: Sense the townsfolk is hiding the full extent of their terror.

**Plot Hooks:**

- The townsfolk begs the party to find a missing loved one whose grave was disturbed last night.
- Strange footprints lead east, deeper into the Parish Grounds.

**Loot:**

- Rusty Lantern (1gp, flickers with will-o'-wisp-like light)
- Fragment of a burial record (mentions names and recent interments)

**Encounters:**

- *roleplay*: Desperate Townsfolk pleads for help, and may share cryptic warnings or direct the party to recent disturbances.
- *exploration*: Examining the area reveals clues about recent grave disturbances and the unnatural quiet that hangs over the crossroads.

**NPCs:**

- Desperate Townsfolk (Fearful, desperate for help but wary of strangers.)

*Connected to: Within area: C02, C03; Fields of Pottsfield / A03*

**DM Guidance:**

Open with tense roleplay: the Desperate Townsfolk can reveal cryptic rumors or tempt the party with information for protection. If players linger, something unseen may scratch at the earth nearby (Perception DC 13). Use the crossroads as an atmospheric hub to set the tone.

### C02 -- Graven Stillhouse

A squat, stone outbuilding partially sunk into the ground. Its warped wooden door hangs askew, and faded carvings of harvest scenes are set into the lintel. Inside, the air is musty and cold. Cracked shelves are stacked with jars of earth and faded burial vestments. A broad central slab--stained and marked with ritual sigils--dominates the room, hinting at recent necromantic rites.

*Danger Level: Medium*

*Accessibility: Requires pushing aside the heavy door; otherwise, unbarred and easily entered.*

>The Graven Stillhouse offers a tense mix of roleplay and danger, allowing the party to interact with a tormented spirit and uncover critical evidence of the curse plaguing Pottsfield.

**Features:**

- **Signs Of Age**: Crumbled mortar, rotting shelves, and faded carvings reveal centuries of weather and neglect.
- **Dust**: Layers of fine, gray dust coat everything, occasionally disturbed by unseen movement.
- **Ritual Slab**: A broad, central stone slab covered in glowing runes and dried blood. The focus of recent dark rites.

**DC Checks:**

- Arcana DC 14: Disrupt the binding runes holding the spirit.
- Investigation DC 13: Notice subtle traces of a recent ritual and the presence of necromantic residue.
- Religion DC 12: Recognize the sigils as a perversion of mourning rites.

**Plot Hooks:**

- The Bound Spirit begs the party to disrupt the runes binding them--doing so may draw hostile attention or reveal hidden lore.
- Etched on the slab is a fragmentary prayer referencing Father Milgrom and a 'harvest of souls.'

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 12 | Disable DC 14 | Trigger DC 12 | Damage: 1d8 necrotic (triggered by disturbing the slab without disrupting the runes first; magical backlash).

**Doors:**

- Main Door -- A warped wooden door, banded in iron, creaks loudly on its hinges.

**Loot:**

- Silver Ritual Knife (25gp, faint necromantic residue)
- Jar of Grave Dust (component for certain spells)
- Set of Burial Vestments (worth 5gp to a collector or local priest)

**Encounters:**

- *roleplay*: Bound Spirit pleads for release, offering cryptic aid or warnings if freed.
- *combat*: Disturbing the runes or attempting to remove objects triggers Crawling Claws attack.
- *exploration*: Careful examination yields clues about the necromantic rituals and the dark influence over Pottsfield.

**NPCs:**

- Bound Spirit (Agonized, fearful, but will try to aid the party if freed.)

**Monsters:**

- Crawling Claws -- Necromantically animated hands emerge from jars and scuttle across the floor if the spirit's bindings are tampered with.

*Connected to: Within area: C01*

**DM Guidance:**

The spirit's release requires an Arcana or Religion check (DC 14). If freed, the Bound Spirit can grant cryptic visions or directions. Disrupting the runes triggers the Crawling Claws attack and alerts other nearby undead.

### C03 -- Witherbone Alcove

A shallow crypt-like alcove cut into the hillside, its entrance framed with twisted yew branches and bone charms. Shadows deepen here even in daylight, and the air is cold and close. The floor is littered with brittle, white roots and scraps of funerary masks. A faint, rhythmic thumping echoes from within--a heartbeat or a distant bell?

*Danger Level: Medium*

*Accessibility: A steep, root-choked path leads to the alcove; passable but treacherous footing.*

>Witherbone Alcove serves as both a test and a reward for the party's choices, linking their actions to the fate of the Parish spirits and hinting at the greater evils lurking deeper beneath Pottsfield.

**Features:**

- **Dust**: Dry, powdery dust swirls in the alcove, faintly phosphorescent in darkness.
- **Signs Of Age**: The alcove walls are pitted and worn, with faded ancestral carvings barely visible.
- **Interesting Architecture**: A fusion of natural roots and mortared stone, the alcove's construction is both organic and funerary.

**DC Checks:**

- Perception DC 12: Sense the faint presence of lingering spirits.
- Arcana DC 12: Identify the bone charms as wards against restless dead.
- Insight DC 13: Understand the cryptic warnings given by the Freed Spirits.

**Plot Hooks:**

- The Freed Spirits beseech the party to stop Father Milgrom and break the curse.
- A spirit whispers a riddle about the way to the hidden crypts beneath the Parish Grounds.

**Doors:**

- Yew-Framed Entrance -- Twisted yew branches and bone fetishes create a narrow, eerie archway.

**Loot:**

- Bone Talisman (functions as a charm against necrotic damage, one use)
- Scrap of a funerary mask (depicts a stylized jack-o'-lantern, possible clue for village traditions)

**Encounters:**

- *roleplay*: Freed Spirits appear if the Bound Spirit was aided, offering cryptic guidance and a minor blessing.
- *combat*: If the Bound Spirit wasn't freed, a Restless Skeleton (with a rune-carved skull) attacks anyone disturbing the alcove.
- *exploration*: The alcove contains hidden clues about Pottsfield's funerary practices and the deeper crypt network.

**NPCs:**

- Freed Spirits (Grateful, somber, desperate for rest.)

**Monsters:**

- Restless Skeleton -- If the Bound Spirit is not yet freed, a single animate skeleton guards this alcove, its skull carved with runes of binding.

*Connected to: Within area: C01; Pottsfield Hearthlands / B01*

**DM Guidance:**

If the party has already freed the Bound Spirit, the Freed Spirits appear and can offer cryptic guidance and a small magical boon. If not, the Restless Skeleton attacks, compelled to defend the alcove. The scraps of funerary masks here can provide clues about Pottsfield's masked rituals.

## PVC001 -- Pottsfield Hearthlands

Nestled among golden fields and twisting cobblestone lanes, the Pottsfield Hearthlands present themselves as a picture-perfect harvest town: lanterns glow from porch hooks, garlands of dried corn hang from eaves, and the air smells of cider, woodsmoke, and rain-dark soil. Yet the charm never quite settles comfortably. Lace curtains twitch too often, conversations die when strangers pass, and the town's cheerful masks seem less like celebration than concealment. The place feels lived-in and ancient, a community built on ritual and reputation, where every smiling face may be hiding grief, guilt, or allegiance to something older than the town itself. That tension is the Hearthlands' defining note--warmth on the surface, watchfulness underneath.

The area's rooms and gathering places all feed into the adventure's central thread of deception and revelation. The town's festival hall anchors the public face of Pottsfield, where council business and autumn revels blur together, making it the ideal stage for the masked intrigue of the Revels and the social maneuvering that drives the plot forward. The undertakers' guildhouse, by contrast, suggests that the dead are being managed with unusual secrecy, hinting at hidden funerary rites, missing records, or quiet bargains struck behind closed doors. The old burial gate and its adjoining grounds tie the Hearthlands to the Shrouded Necropolis, while the more civic spaces connect onward to the Parishlands, letting the DM move the party naturally from polite suspicion to graveyard secrets and then deeper into the adventure's darker heart.

Pottsfield's key figures are as memorable as its lanterns. Deputy Silas Cragg gives the town a stern, practical edge, the sort of man who knows every family line and every buried scandal, while Widow Elsie Tallow lingers near the burial grounds like a keeper of old mourning customs and older warnings. Within the festival halls, masked revelers mingle among the townsfolk, their identities obscured just enough to suggest that some of them are not mere celebrants at all, but agents of the necropolis's influence or conspirators in the town's hidden rites. The area also opens cleanly into the surrounding adventure: one path leads toward the Parishlands and the town's more openly spiritual concerns, while another descends toward the Shrouded Necropolis, where the Hearthlands' secrets harden into something colder, older, and far more dangerous.

**Area Type:** Town | **Recommended Level:** 3

### B01 -- Gravebinders' Assembly

Once a stone barn, this assembly hall is the headquarters for Pottsfield's secretive undertakers' guild. Rows of rustic benches and faded banners bearing cryptic emblems flank a wide hearth, its embers cold despite the town's chill. Shelves of bone-handled tools and ledgers line the walls. The air carries the scent of old earth and dried rosemary.

*Danger Level: Medium*

*Accessibility: Normal access during daylight; after dusk, only guild members can enter without raising suspicion.*

>The party may gain critical information about Pottsfield's necromantic secrets and the guild's true role if they earn Cragg's trust or search the room.

**Features:**

- **Dust**: A heavy mantle of undisturbed dust covers ledgers and benches, except the hearth area--recently disturbed.
- **Guild Emblems**: Faded banners display a scythe entwined with a lantern--symbol of the Gravebinders.

**DC Checks:**

- Perception DC 13: Notice the faint outline of the hearth trapdoor.
- Investigation DC 15: Decipher the ledger's coded entries.

**Plot Hooks:**

- A cryptic ledger hints at townsfolk who died but were seen alive later.
- Deputy Cragg warns of trespassers in the Parish Grounds vanishing without a trace.
- A locked chest beneath the hearth is rumored to contain forbidden rites.

**Doors:**

- Main Hall Door -- Heavy oak double doors, weathered and creaking on iron hinges. -- Locked (DC 12) -- Key: Guild Key
- Hearth Trapdoor -- Concealed beneath the hearth's ashes, a sturdy trapdoor with a brass ring. -- Locked (DC 15) -- Key: Deputy's Brass Key

**Loot:**

- Set of bone-handled grave tools (set, 5 gp)
- Ledger listing names and odd symbols (clue)
- Phial of gravebloom oil (10 gp, faintly magical, necromancy)

**Encounters:**

- Deputy Silas Cragg interrogates the party about their purpose.
- A hidden chest (DC 15 Perception) contains ritual scrolls (see loot).

**NPCs:**

## THG001 -- The Reaping Fields

### D03 -- Silent Fork

A fork in the forest path where three moss-choked trails diverge. At the center stands a crumbling stone altar, its surface stained and engraved with cryptic symbols that pulse faintly in the mist. The silence here is absolute--no birds, no wind, just heartbeats and the distant whisper of lost souls.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: Easy to reach along the main woodland path, but the silence and faint magical aura unsettle even the bravest travelers.*

>A crossroads of fate, where truth, danger, and the next steps in the Veilwood mystery converge.

**Features:**

- **Interesting Architecture**: A weathered altar, covered in cryptic runes and old bloodstains, dominates the crossroads.

**DC Checks:**

- Religion DC 12: Identify the altar as a site of necromantic rituals tied to the Pottsfield curse.
- Medicine DC 10: Notice Father Milgrom's spiritual injuries.
- Strength DC 18: Lift or move the stone slab.
- Perception DC 13: Spot the hidden crawlspace beneath the slab.

**Plot Hooks:**

- Father Milgrom can explain the altar's significance, the true nature of Pottsfield's affliction, and direct the party to the next leg of their quest.
- The altar's runes match those on the stones of Graven Summit, suggesting a ritual connection.

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 14 | Disable DC 15 | Trigger DC 12 | Damage: 2d4 poison (thorny roots beneath the slab lash out when moved)

**Doors:**

- Stone Slab -- A heavy, moss-covered stone slab set into the ground beside the altar. It covers a root-choked crawlspace. -- Trapped

**Loot:**

- Consecrated vial of holy water (created by Father Milgrom; useful against undead)
- Altar etching kit (can make a rubbing of the runes for study elsewhere)
- Milgrom's journal fragment (details the true nature of the curse and hints at the next step--seeking the heart of the Veilwood)

**Encounters:**

- *roleplay*: Father Milgrom offers lore, guidance, and solace, but is wary until trust is earned.
- *combat*: The Veilwood Husk attacks if the altar is desecrated or Milgrom is threatened.

**NPCs:**

- Father Milgrom (Penitent, desperate, eager to help (if convinced of the party's good intent))

**Monsters:**

- Veilwood Husk -- A twisted, undead guardian animated by the altar's magic. It remains dormant until intruders disturb the altar or threaten Father Milgrom.

*Connected to: Within area: D02*

**DM Guidance:**

Include Father Milgrom as a key roleplay encounter. If the party soothes or aids him, he provides guidance and a holy relic. The Veilwood Husk only attacks if the altar is disturbed or Milgrom is threatened. The crossroads paths can be explored for additional wilderness encounters or shortcuts.
