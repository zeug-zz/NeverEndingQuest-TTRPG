# Xhalruuns Masquerade

## Introduction

### Module Overview
At the edge of Numillian lies The Paradoxical Crossroads, a town where streets loop like half-remembered dreams and fate itself seems to misplace its own footsteps; here, the adventurers arrive into a web of uncanny synchronicities, whispered omens, and a hidden city waiting beyond the Paradoxical Gates. This module unfolds as a city-spanning mystery of unraveling reality, masked conspiracies, and impossible places, offering the Dungeon Master 17 NPCs to bring the intrigue to life, 24 plot points to guide the tale, 6 vivid locations to explore, and 16 creature stat blocks to menace, mislead, and test the party as they chase the truth through a world coming apart at the seams.

### The Story So Far
The adventurers begin in the Paradoxical Crossroads, where Keeper Veyron warns that luck and reality are fraying like old thread in a storm. As they investigate looping streets, missing memories, and strange disappearances, they learn that the town's geomantic anchors have been sabotaged and that a masked cabal known as the Luckbinder's circle seeks to seize control of destiny itself. Their choices amid midnight rites and hidden gatherings determine whether the Paradoxical Gates steady into passage or burst open into chaos, setting the tone for the trials ahead.

Beyond the gates, the party enters the Veiled Districts, where masked faces hide secret loyalties and disappearances deepen into a conspiracy tied to a shadow entity seeking purchase in Numillian. From there the road bends into the Unraveling Wards, where Mira the Shardseer is trapped amid broken magic and Azarel Moros offers dangerous solutions that could restore the district or remake it in a stranger image. The journey then descends into the Netherwoven Warrens, an ancient undercity labyrinth where a dimensional rift gnaws at the foundations of the realm, before rising into the Shrouded Psiumbra, where the undead Umbral Chorus prepares a psychic convergence to merge dream and waking world.

At last, the trail leads to The Shifting Confluence, where alternate timelines flicker at the edges of sight and a stolen fragment of Numillian's core is hunted by rival powers. There the party pursues Syllik the Mirrorfoot through ever-changing streets toward a final crossroads of unreality, where the fate of the fragment, the city's restoration, and Numillian's very future hang in the balance. Across every district, the central conflict remains the same: reality is unraveling, and only the adventurers' courage, judgment, and alliances can decide whether Numillian is mended, transformed, or lost to the dark between worlds.

### Running the Adventure
This adventure is designed for a party of 3rd to 5th level characters and plays best with a standard group of four to five adventurers, though a smaller or larger party can be accommodated with care. Before running it, the DM should read ahead through the major faction turns, decide which NPCs are allies or antagonists in each district, and be ready to emphasize investigation, social intrigue, and shifting reality as much as combat, since the module's pleasures lie in its mysteries as much as in its monsters.

# Plot Overview

At the edge of Numillian stands the Paradoxical Crossroads, where streets loop back on themselves, memories slip like water through cracked glass, and every whispered direction seems to lead toward doom or destiny. When a band of adventurers arrives beneath a sky heavy with omen, they find a city unspooling from within: masked cabals barter in secret, hidden rituals tug at the seams of fate, and a shadowed intelligence stirs beneath lantern light and stone. To mend the unraveling, they must navigate a maze of conspiracies, vanishing districts, psychic masquerades, and impossible labyrinths, gathering fragments of truth and power before reality tears open for good. But in Numillian, every alliance has a cost, every mask hides a hunger, and the path to salvation may demand choosing who gets to shape the world when the last thread finally breaks.

### PP001 -- Arrival at The Paradoxical Crossroads

The adventurers arrive at The Paradoxical Crossroads (TPG001), a town on the threshold of Numillian, renowned for uncanny synchronicities and crossroads of fate. Keeper Veyron greets them with cryptic warnings: the fabric of luck and reality is unraveling, causing looping streets, lost memories, and disappearances. Solving these mysteries is the only way forward, and the party soon hears whispers of a hidden city just beyond the Paradoxical Gates.

### PP002 -- Mapping the Unstable Streets

Using clues from Old Grem's map and local guidance, the party investigates the fluctuating town layout and discovers that the crossroads are geomantic anchors for Numillian's reality. One anchor has been sabotaged, and a masked figure has been seen meddling in forbidden places. Pursuing the figure, the party uncovers hints that the sabotage is connected to the barrier protecting the Hidden City beyond the Paradoxical Gates.

### PP003 -- The Serendipity Conspiracy

The party uncovers a secret society of masked dignitaries--the Luckbinder's cabal--who seek to harness the crossroads' power. Preparing for a midnight ritual to seize control of fate, the cabal's plans threaten to further destabilize the gateway into Numillian. The party must infiltrate their gathering, navigate intrigue, and choose whether to aid, expose, or oppose the society, as their decision will influence who controls the passage through the Paradoxical Gates.

### PP004 -- Midnight at the Shifting Crossroads

As midnight strikes, reality bends and the Luckbinder enacts the ritual at the crossroads. The party must disrupt the ceremony--fighting fate-spirits and masked conspirators--while the city's shifting energies threaten chaos. Their success or failure determines if the Paradoxical Gates stabilize, allowing passage deeper into Numillian, or if uncontrolled chaos spreads, complicating all future journeys.

### PP005 -- Shadows Over the Veiled Districts

With the Paradoxical Gates stabilized or thrown into flux, the party enters the Veiled Districts (TMQ001), a maze of masked denizens and shifting alliances. Disappearances plague the district, and local authorities and masked factions quietly request the adventurers' subtle assistance. The party learns the district's instability is connected to the citywide unraveling and must tread carefully to avoid open conflict.

### PP006 -- Labyrinth of Lies

The party's investigation leads deeper into the Veiled Districts, where coded messages, secret meetings, and masked societies abound. They must determine which cabal holds the key to the disappearances, all while learning that these events are linked to an entity attempting to anchor itself within Numillian. The party's alliances here will affect their ability to stop the city's unravelling.

### PP007 -- Revelation Beneath the Lanterns

The party infiltrates a masked ball beneath the Veiled Districts, where the cabal's leaders gather to conduct a ritual. They learn the disappearances are part of a larger scheme to anchor a shadow entity--a threat tied to the unraveling of Numillian itself. The party can expose, join, or sabotage the cabal's plans, and their choice will ripple into the city's fate.

### PP008 -- Masks Fall, Shadows Rise

As the cabal's ritual begins, the party must act: disrupt the rite, confront the shadow entity's emissary, or forge a new order. The climax blends combat and social maneuvering, with desperate NPCs vying for power. The outcome determines whether the Veiled Districts stabilize, descend into chaos, or become a bastion of secrecy.

### PP009 -- Shattered Threshold: Entering the Unraveling Wards

With the Veiled Districts' fate determined, the party is summoned to the Unraveling Wards (TUD001)--a quarter warped by reality-defying forces spreading from the heart of Numillian. Their objective: locate Mira the Shardseer, who may hold the key to the city's unraveling. The party immediately faces the dangers of spatial chaos and fractured magic.

### PP010 -- The Spiral Heart: Investigating the Rupture

Tracing Mira's trail, the party navigates labyrinthine streets to the Spiral Heart--the disruption's epicenter. They confront frayed magic and hostile entities, meeting Azarel Moros, a scholar with his own agenda. The party learns that the unraveling is no accident, and must decide whether to trust Azarel or strike out on their own toward the source.

### PP011 -- Fractured Alliances: The Choice of Restoration

At the heart of the Wards, the party finds Mira ensnared in an arcane lattice. Azarel and a cabal of reality-meddlers offer a dangerous solution: destroy the wards and remake reality, or attempt an ancient ritual to restore the district. Their choice will determine who holds power as Numillian's core grows more unstable.

### PP012 -- Echoes of Reality: The Climax and Aftermath

The party enacts their plan as the unraveling peaks--reality bends, time fractures, and hostile entities surge forth. Their battle and ritual efforts determine whether the Wards are restored, transformed, or lost forever. The aftermath changes the arcane and political landscape, as Numillian's core grows even more unstable.

### PP013 -- Whispers Beneath the Stones: The Descent into the Netherwoven Warrens

Summoned to Illune's End, the party investigates citizens vanishing and eldritch fissures pulsing with energy. All signs point to the Netherwoven Warrens (TSU001), an ancient labyrinth below the city, serving as a fractured anchor for a burgeoning dimensional rift. The party must find the hidden entrance, as the instability threatens to spill forth into Numillian.

### PP014 -- Frayed Realities: Navigating the Shifting Labyrinth

Within the Netherwoven Warrens, the party faces twisted corridors, gravity-defying chambers, and bizarre monsters. They discover the Warrens are a fractured anchor for a dimensional rift, their progress complicated by reality-bending traps and ghostly echoes. Stabilizing the Warrens is critical to preventing Numillian's total collapse.

### PP015 -- The Weaver in the Shadows: Confronting the Rift's Guardian

At the heart of the Warrens, the party faces the Arcane Weaver, a being half-bound to reality. It offers a bargain: help it manifest in exchange for closing the rift, or fight to prevent catastrophe. This confrontation's outcome will echo across Numillian, influencing the final unraveling at the city's core.

### PP016 -- Threads Unbound: Sealing or Shattering the Netherwoven Warrens

With the rift's fate decided, the party must stabilize the labyrinth or escape its collapse as reality warps chaotically. Success may seal the Warrens and restore peace; failure could unleash horrors upon Numillian, hastening the city's unraveling.

### PP017 -- Arrival at the Shrouded Psiumbra

With the underground threat contained or unleashed, the party travels to the Shrouded Psiumbra (TMFMH001), a district shimmering with illusions and psychic energies. Here, the Umbral Chorus--an undead mind flayer cabal--seeks to complete a ritual merging dream and reality. The party must discover the mind flayer plot and recover the fractured memory core, a key element needed to stabilize Numillian itself.

### PP018 -- Through the Maze of Illusions

To reach the Psiumbra's heart, the party navigates a labyrinth of shifting corridors and psychic traps. Enchanted masqueraders and enthralled guards block their way, and they learn of the impending 'Midnight Convergence'--the Umbral Chorus's ritual to merge realities. Securing the memory core before the ritual is critical to restoring Numillian's stability.

### PP019 -- Masquerade of Madness

At the grand masquerade, the party must blend in with masked dignitaries, mind-controlled commoners, and lurking mind flayers. Social intrigue, sabotage, and stealth are key as they attempt to gather the ritual's details, identify key cultists, and decide whom to trust. Their actions here will determine their ability to disrupt the ritual and recover the memory core.

### PP020 -- The Midnight Convergence

The party confronts the Umbral Chorus as the Midnight Convergence begins. Reality buckles under psychic energies; illusions become dangerously real. The party must overcome both physical and mental trials, defeat the mind flayer masterminds, and secure the memory core. Their choices determine if the district is freed, warped, or transformed into something stranger still.

### PP021 -- Arrival at The Shifting Confluence

With the memory core in hand, the party's journey takes them to The Shifting Confluence (TNU001), where reality is thin and alternate timelines flicker at the edge of perception. Here, a fragment of Numillian's core needed to complete the city's restoration has surfaced, but is being hunted by forces loyal to the mind flayer and by a reality-warped thief.

### PP022 -- The Fractal Chase

Clues lead the party to the heart of The Shifting Confluence, where Syllik the Mirrorfoot, a reality-warped thief, has stolen the Numillian fragment. The party must navigate ever-morphing streets, pursue Syllik through alternate versions of the town, and avoid rival agents sent by the mind flayer to recover the fragment.

### PP023 -- Crossroads of Unreality

The chase culminates at the Confluence's epicenter, where Syllik attempts to open a permanent rift with the Numillian fragment. The party must confront Syllik and rival agents in a battlefield of shifting terrain. Their approach--combat, bargain, or cunning--will shape the fate of the fragment and the Confluence.

### PP024 -- Restoration or Ruin

With Syllik defeated or bargained with, the party must decide how to use the Numillian fragment. Their choices can stabilize the Confluence and heal Numillian, or harness its power at great risk. The consequences will echo throughout the city, shaping Numillian's destiny and setting the stage for future adventures.

# Locations

## TMFMH001 -- The Shrouded Psiumbra

The Shrouded Psiumbra is a district that seems to breathe in whispers and exhale delirium. Its labyrinthine corridors shimmer beneath veils of illusion, where gilded arches, drifting banners, and mirrored surfaces never quite hold the same shape twice. Sinister music leaks through the walls like perfume through silk, and muffled laughter follows intruders from unseen balconies, making every step feel both ceremonial and wrong. This is a place of cultivated decadence warped by psychic influence: beautiful enough to invite admiration, uncanny enough to inspire dread. The district's distinctive quality lies in the way it weaponizes perception itself, turning opulence into a trap and every reflection into a lie.

The area's rooms connect like a descent into a larger conspiracy. New arrivals are drawn through the welcoming splendor of the antechamber, where the first hints of the masquerade's true nature appear behind the elegance. From there, the shifting corridor of contradictory histories reinforces the adventure's central mystery, suggesting that Numillian's past has been edited, rewritten, or devoured by something far older and hungrier. Deeper still, the vault of psychic records and buried memories reveals the stakes of the plot: the district is not merely a social theater, but a repository of stolen truths and fractured identities tied to the broader events of the adventure's arrival sequence. The Shrouded Psiumbra serves as the threshold between the more accessible Veiled Districts and the deeper intrigues beyond, and its passages ultimately guide the party onward into The Veiled Districts, while also remaining reachable from elsewhere in that same shadowed network.

The district is ruled, or at least curated, by a Masquerade Steward whose polished courtesy conceals predatory intent, while a Masked Duelist patrols the shifting halls as both enforcer and spectacle. Illusionary Shadow Dancers haunt the entrance like living stagecraft, and minor mind flayer thralls drift through the corridors as obedient attendants to the unseen will behind the masquerade. In the depths, a Mind Flayer Archivist tends the vault's forbidden lore, guarded by spectral memory wraiths that rise from stolen recollections and unresolved terror. Together, these inhabitants make the Shrouded Psiumbra feel like a court of elegant nightmares: a place where every conversation is a test, every performance a threat, and every door leads either deeper into revelation or into the waiting jaws of madness.

**Area Type:** Mixed | **Recommended Level:** 7

### AC01 -- Veilpiercer Antechamber

This sumptuous antechamber welcomes newcomers to the Mind Flayer's Masquerade Hall. Gilded arches soar overhead, veiled in gauzy illusions that shift with every glance. The very air thrums with psychic resonance, and shadowy figures in elaborate masks mingle beneath opalescent chandeliers whose crystal drops flicker with images of forgotten faces. Subtle illusions distort depth and distance, making navigation both mesmerizing and perilous.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: Normal access from the district's main corridor; the ever-shifting walls might disorient newcomers.*

>The party is subtly assessed upon entry, with the steward offering information or warnings based on their actions. The antechamber sets the hall's eerie tone and offers clues to deeper dangers.

**Features:**

- **Interesting Architecture**: Tall gilded arches and mirrored pillars refract light into impossible patterns, distorting space.
- **Illusionary Chandeliers**: Crystals flicker with shifting faces and scenes from distant memories.

**DC Checks:**

- Perception DC 16: Notice that some of the guests are not real, but psychic projections.
- Arcana DC 15: Identify the presence of a subtle mind-affecting field that clouds memory.

**Plot Hooks:**

- The steward hints at a 'guest of honor' whose presence is disrupting the masquerade.
- Whispers among the guests mention a vault where the mind flayers store their most coveted memories.

**Doors:**

- Opalescent Archway -- A tall archway overlaid with shimmering illusions, leading east deeper into the Hall.

**Loot:**

- Phantasmal Token (can reveal one illusion in the Psiumbra, single use)
- Fine masquerade mask (25 gp, social advantage in this district)
- Psychic Residue Gemstone (worth 50 gp to collectors of the arcane)

**Encounters:**

- If the party fails to blend in or draws attention, the illusionary dancers attempt to entrap them within psychic mazes.

**NPCs:**

- Masquerade Steward (Polite and enigmatic, loyal to the Hall's true masters.)

**Monsters:**

- Illusionary Shadow Dancers -- Semi-corporeal entities spawned from lingering psychic energies, disrupting intruders' senses and movements.

*Connected to: Within area: AC02*

**DM Guidance:**

Set the tone of shifting reality--emphasize confusing perspectives and atmospheric tension. If the party lingers or acts suspiciously, the shadow dancers coalesce to observe or challenge them. Illusions may briefly show NPCs the party recognizes, or enemies from their past.

### AC02 -- Hall of Shifting Truths

A broad corridor lined with ever-morphing murals and floating banners, each depicting contradictory versions of Numillian's history. The floor undulates slightly, as if alive, and reality itself feels thin. Conversations echo with double-meanings and psychic aftershocks. Here, mind games and psychic duels are as common as masked balls.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: Open from the Antechamber, but progress east requires solving the duelist's riddle or bypassing the trap.*

>This hall tests the party's wits and resolve. Success here opens hidden paths and reveals truths about the hall's ever-shifting nature.

**Features:**

- **Interesting Architecture**: The corridor's width and decor shift subtly, never appearing exactly the same twice.
- **Signs Of Age**: Some murals flicker to reveal much older and darker histories beneath.

**DC Checks:**

- Investigation DC 15: Find inconsistencies in the murals that hint at the secret door.
- Insight DC 17: Discern that the duelist is testing more than physical skill--he's probing for psychic strength.

**Plot Hooks:**

- Murals hint at a secret route to the Mindsunder Vault, only visible to those who solve the duelist's riddle.
- A psychic tremor reveals that an important guest is being 'rewritten' in the next chamber.

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 16 | Disable DC 18 | Trigger DC 15 | Damage: 2d6 psychic (shifting hallucinations overwhelm the mind if the Mural Gate is forced without solving the riddle)

**Doors:**

- Mural Gate -- A door disguised as part of a shifting mural, leading further east. -- Locked (DC 18) -- Key: Riddle Solution
- Antechamber Entry -- Open arch leading back to the Veilpiercer Antechamber.

**Loot:**

- Memory Fragment (consumable, gain fleeting insight into the Hall's secrets)
- Silvered rapier with an inscription in Deep Speech (75 gp)
- Scroll of Detect Thoughts

**Encounters:**

- The duelist challenges a party member to a contest of riddles or mental fortitude; success gains safe passage and a clue, failure risks psychic backlash.

**NPCs:**

- Masked Duelist (Challenging but not overtly hostile; respects cleverness and mental strength.)

**Monsters:**

- Minor Mind Flayer Thralls -- Enthralled party guests, appearing as revelers until provoked.

*Connected to: Within area: AC01, AC03*

**DM Guidance:**

Encourage players to engage in social or psychic tests (contests of Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma). The Hall can subtly alter facts or memories, keeping players on edge. If players lose a contest, introduce minor temporary conditions such as confusion or fleeting paranoia.

### AC03 -- Mindsunder Vault

Deep within the Masquerade Hall, this chamber feels colder and heavier. Walls of obsidian glass reflect not only visitors' faces but their innermost fears and aspirations. Psychic motes drift in the still air, distorting reality. This vault stores the 'prized' memories, secrets, and psychic remnants harvested from revelers--some contained in crystalline reliquaries, others haunting the space as faint, tormented echoes.

*Danger Level: Very High*

*Accessibility: Accessible from the Hall of Shifting Truths, but the vault door poses a significant barrier.*

>The vault is a crucible of memory and temptation. The archivist may become an ally or formidable foe, and the psychic treasures here can change the party's future choices.

**Features:**

- **Dust**: A thin layer of silvery dust covers the floor, glittering with psychic motes.
- **Obsidian Mirrors**: Reflect not only physical form, but fragments of memory and emotion.

**DC Checks:**

- Arcana DC 16: Identify the wraiths as psychic remnants, not true undead.
- History DC 18: Recognize the reliquaries as containing memories from powerful Numillian figures.

**Plot Hooks:**

- The archivist offers a deal--trade a memory for information about Numillian's illusions.
- A spectral voice pleads for release, offering to reveal secrets of the vault if freed from psychic bondage.

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 17 | Disable DC 20 | Trigger DC 17 | Damage: 3d6 psychic (from the Vault Door's defensive enchantment)

**Doors:**

- Obsidian Vault Door -- A heavy, seamless panel of black glass, etched with writhing psychic runes. Opens only to the reliquary key or by solving a psychic puzzle. -- Locked (DC 20) -- Trapped -- Key: Reliquary Key
- Mural Gate -- A secret door leading west back to the Hall of Shifting Truths.

**Loot:**

- Memory Prism (contains a vital clue to the mind flayer's true motives)
- Reliquary Key (opens a hidden cache elsewhere in the Hall)
- Psionic Focus Crystal (acts as a spellcasting focus for psychic magic)

**Encounters:**

- The mind flayer archivist offers to trade secrets for precious memories or tries to trap intruders within the vault.

**NPCs:**

- Mind Flayer Archivist (Coldly curious; prefers to negotiate or manipulate unless threatened.)

**Monsters:**

- Spectral Memory Wraiths -- Remnants of lost minds, twisted into aggressive psychic entities.

*Connected to: Within area: AC02; The Veiled Districts / AK01*

**DM Guidance:**

The archivist is a formidable social and magical adversary, preferring to manipulate or strike bargains. If attacked, the wraiths are summoned for defense. Use the memory trade as a roleplay opportunity, with meaningful consequences for what the party offers.

## TMQ001 -- The Veiled Districts

The Veiled Districts breathe like a living secret: lanterns sway above narrow alleys, incense drifts from hidden braziers, and every doorway is framed by masks that seem less decorative than accusatory. This is a place where identity is currency and silence is a language all its own. The district's distinctive charm lies in its constant uncertainty--faces are hidden, loyalties are obscured, and even the architecture feels complicit, with gilded arches, mirrored corridors, and mosaic floors that shift underfoot as though the streets themselves are deciding what to reveal. To walk here is to feel watched by strangers who may be allies, informants, or something far stranger still.

The district's chambers are threaded directly into the adventure's intrigue, each one tightening the knot around the plot of **Shadows Over the Veiled Districts**. The grand masked entryway serves as a social threshold where the party may be drawn into delicate negotiations or subtle warnings, while the mirror-lined passages turn rumor into danger, offering glimpses of hidden truths and possible futures that hint at the larger conspiracy. Deeper within, the unmasking chamber strips away deception entirely, making it a natural place for revelations, confrontations, and the exposure of those who have come cloaked in false identities. These spaces do not merely decorate the mystery--they advance it, forcing the characters to decide whom to trust and what truths are worth the cost of revealing.

Among the district's most notable figures is **Seraphine of the Silver Veil**, whose poised presence suggests both patronage and peril, and **Malk the Whisper**, a shadowbroker whose secrets travel faster than footsteps through the alleys. The halls also teem with **Mirror Phantoms**, eerie manifestations that seem born from reflection, regret, and half-spoken lies, while masked dignitaries gather behind polished faces to bargain over matters they would never dare name aloud. The Veiled Districts connect outward to the broader adventure like a hidden hinge: they lead onward to **The Shrouded Psiumbra** and **The Shifting Confluence**, while also being reachable from those same regions, making the district a crossroads of intrigue where clues, agents, and consequences can flow in both directions.

**Area Type:** Mixed | **Recommended Level:** 5

### AH01 -- Shifting Veil Foyer

A grand, lantern-lit chamber of gilded archways and shifting mosaic floors. The air is heavy with incense, and countless masks--carved from wood, bone, and crystal--hang from the walls, each seeming to watch visitors with silent scrutiny. Every step echoes with whispers of secrets, and those entering feel as though their own faces are momentarily not their own. This is the arrival point for those hoping to conduct business or seek intrigue within the Masked Quarters.

*Danger Level: Medium*

*Accessibility: Normal access; guarded by Seraphine's subtle scrutiny.*

>The party enters the Masked Quarters through the Shifting Veil Foyer, where they meet Seraphine of the Silver Veil and are offered their first choices in navigating the district's intrigues.

**Features:**

- **Interesting Architecture**: The room's mosaic floor shifts subtly underfoot, making it appear as if patterns are rearranging to form different faces.
- **Mask Gallery**: A collection of hundreds of masks, each with a unique expression. Some are enchanted to shift their features when unobserved.

**DC Checks:**

- Insight DC 12: Notice Seraphine's subtle signals to hidden allies.
- Investigation DC 13: Find a concealed message etched behind a mask.

**Plot Hooks:**

- Seraphine offers to share a secret about the district's ruler if the party delivers a veiled message to a contact deeper in the Masked Quarters.
- A masked noble seeks protection during a clandestine rendezvous nearby, promising information about Numillian's illusions.

**Doors:**

- East Archway -- An ornate arch of dark wood inlaid with silver, flanked by heavy velvet curtains.

**Loot:**

- Silver Mask of Illusory Glamour (allows the wearer to cast Disguise Self once per long rest)
- Incense Pouch of Scented Shadows (can be used to create a 10-foot cube of magical darkness for 1 minute, once per day)

**NPCs:**

- Seraphine of the Silver Veil (Cautiously helpful, but always watching for leverage.)

*Connected to: Within area: AH02; The Shrouded Psiumbra / AO03*

**DM Guidance:**

Seraphine is the party's initial point of contact for information and access; adjust her responses based on how much the party reveals about themselves. She will not allow obvious threats or overt violence.

### AH02 -- Hall of Fractured Truths

A long, shadowed corridor lined with fractured mirrors and mosaic tiles. Each reflective surface distorts reality, showing glimpses of possible futures or hidden truths. Whispered voices echo along the hall, and anyone passing through may find their own secrets laid bare. Dust carpets the corners, hinting at seldom-trodden paths. The Hall is a crossroads for clandestine dealings, where information and rumor are as valuable as gold.

*Danger Level: Medium*

*Accessibility: Accessible by foot; the mirrors may confuse navigation or reveal unwanted secrets.*

>The party must navigate the hall without falling prey to its illusions or inciting the wrath of its mirror phantoms, and may bargain with Malk the Whisper for crucial intelligence.

**Features:**

- **Signs Of Age**: The corridor's mirrors and tiles are pitted and cracked, hinting at old magic and long-kept secrets.
- **Dust**: Dust carpets the floor and veils the corners, muffling footsteps and hinting at neglect.
- **Interesting Architecture**: The hall's walls curve subtly, creating an illusion of infinite reflection and depth.

**DC Checks:**

- Perception DC 13: Notice a mirror phantom lurking just out of sight.
- Arcana DC 14: Identify the magical nature of the mirrors and how to avoid triggering them.

**Plot Hooks:**

- Malk offers to reveal a masked dignitary's secret in exchange for a rumor from the party's homeland.
- A mirror phantom drops a cryptic clue about the mind flayer's whereabouts when defeated.

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 14 | Disable DC 15 | Trigger DC 12 | Damage: 2d8 psychic (memory drain from a cursed mirror if touched without care)

**Doors:**

- West Door to Foyer -- A narrow, silvery door with a cracked glass inset.
- East Door to Alcove -- A tall, mirrored door with mosaic inlays that shimmer with illusory light.

**Loot:**

- Shard of Mirror Truth (once, shows a single hidden fact or enemy's secret)
- Small pouch of gold-veined dust (acts as a spell component for divination magic)

**Encounters:**

- If the party discusses secrets aloud, two mirror phantoms appear and attack, attempting to cloud minds and consume memories.

**NPCs:**

- Malk the Whisper (Evasive and cryptic, but intrigued by clever questions.)

**Monsters:**

- Mirror Phantoms -- Insubstantial specters that emerge from the fractured mirrors when secrets are spoken aloud, attempting to drain memories and sow confusion.

*Connected to: Within area: AH01, AH03*

**DM Guidance:**

Malk is an excellent source of exposition and clues, but never gives a straight answer. If the party fights the mirror phantoms, increase their confusion effects if secrets are loudly discussed.

### AH03 -- Illusion's End Alcove

A secluded, circular chamber at the end of the Masked Quarters' main path. The floor is a single, unbroken disc of black glass that reflects only what is real--illusions and magical disguises vanish upon entering. The walls are draped in heavy velvet, and a single pedestal at the center holds a mask carved from obsidian. This alcove is where bargains are finalized and identities revealed, for better or worse.

*Danger Level: Medium*

*Accessibility: Restricted; the door is usually locked and guarded by both magical and social barriers.*

>The party's decisions here may set the course for their alliances and opposition within Numillian, and secrets revealed in this alcove will echo throughout the city's intrigues.

**Features:**

- **Interesting Architecture**: The chamber's black glass floor and obsidian pedestal create an atmosphere of stark honesty and finality.
- **Illusion Suppression**: All illusions and magical disguises are stripped away within the alcove.

**DC Checks:**

- History DC 14: Recall the traditions of the Masked Quarters and the role of this alcove.
- Insight DC 15: Discern which dignitary is most receptive to the party's aims.

## TUD001 -- The Unraveling Wards

The Unraveling Wards feel like a city caught mid-scream, a once-vibrant district now folded in on itself by some patient, malignant spellwork. Streets coil into impossible loops, facades split like cracked porcelain, and every stone seems to vibrate with a low, teeth-rattling hum that makes conversation feel like a trespass. Shadows move wrong here--too long, too late, or in places where no light should cast them at all--and the air itself seems frayed, as though reality has been picked apart at the seams. What makes the area distinctive is not simply its ruin, but its instability: the district is less a place than a wound in the city, a place where memory, magic, and architecture all unravel together.

This region serves as the adventure's first true breach, the shattered threshold through which the party enters the larger mystery. Its twisting chambers and broken streets do not merely provide obstacles; they echo the plot's central theme of things being unmade and remade into stranger forms. The crossroads at its heart acts as a pivot point, linking the party to other branches of the adventure and ultimately opening the way toward the Netherwoven Warrens below. The alcoves and niches tucked off the main thoroughfares each reveal another facet of the same corruption--whispers of hidden truths, distorted recollections, and reflections that may expose what the party has lost, or what has been taken from them.

A few unsettling figures haunt these ruins. Azarel Moros stands at the district's broken center like a scholar or conspirator who has stayed too long at the edge of forbidden knowledge, while Mira the Shardseer and Raxil the Doubter linger in the side chambers, each offering uneasy insight at a cost. Around them drift raveling wraiths, illusory specters, and mirrorborn echoes--monsters born from the wards' collapse and the city's broken memory. Together they make the area feel inhabited by both the living and the half-remembered dead. From here, the adventure spreads outward into adjacent quarters and downward into the Netherwoven Warrens, making the Unraveling Wards both a warning and a gateway: the place where the party first learns that the city's corruption is deeper, older, and far more deliberate than it first appears.

**Area Type:** Mixed | **Recommended Level:** 6

### AQ01 -- Veilwoven Crossroads

The heart of the Unraveling Wards, the Veilwoven Crossroads is a fractured junction where four ancient streets converge. The cobbles here spiral in impossible Moebius loops, and the remnants of once-grand archways now float inches above the ground, humming with destabilizing energy. As the party enters, their shadows lengthen and flicker even when no light source changes. At the center rises a cracked, rune-inscribed plinth, pulsing with erratic magical energies.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: The Crossroads are open but hazardous: magical surges can bar passage without warning, and the layout can change unexpectedly.*

>At the heart of the Unraveling Wards, the party meets Azarel Moros and faces the first test of the district's unstable magic. Here, they can gain critical information about the area's dangers and hints at the hidden structure beneath the city.

**Features:**

- **Dust**: The dust here sparkles with magical residue, swirling unnaturally in the air.
- **Signs Of Age**: Cracks and scorched marks from failed magical experiments mar every surface.
- **Interesting Architecture**: Archways float, walls twist subtly, and some stones seem to hum with living energy.
- **Runic Plinth**: An ancient stone pedestal inscribed with shifting runes, central to the district's magical fabric.

**DC Checks:**

- Arcana DC 17: Stabilize the plinth and reduce magical hazards.
- Investigation DC 15: Locate hidden runic patterns and clues about the district's design.
- Perception DC 13: Hear the distant wailing of the wraiths before they arrive.

**Plot Hooks:**

- Azarel Moros pleads for the party to help anchor the runes at the crossroads, warning that the entire district may collapse if left unattended.
- Clues etched into the plinth hint at a hidden chamber beneath the crossroads, accessible only when the wards' energies align.
- A tattered note in Azarel's possession references a secret held in the 'Fractured Memory Niche.'

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 15 | Disable DC 17 | Trigger DC 14 | Damage: Random wild magic surge (choose an effect or use Wild Magic Surge table; typically minor, but can be dangerous).

**Doors:**

- Shifting South Archway -- A fractured stone arch, flickering between open and blocked by veils of blue energy. -- Trapped
- East Spiral Door -- A wooden door warped into a spiral, the handle only appears when touched. -- Locked (DC 15) -- Key: Shard of Stabilized Reality (found elsewhere)

**Loot:**

- Wand of Reality Stitching (3 charges, can stabilize magical surges)
- Fragmented Arcane Focus (usable as a spellcasting focus, grants +1 on checks to resist illusion magic in this area)
- Scroll: Map of the Hidden City (shows safe paths through the Unraveling Districts)

**Encounters:**

- *combat*: 2 Raveling Wraiths assault the party if magical activity draws their attention.
- *roleplay*: Azarel Moros requests assistance and offers lore about the wards.

**NPCs:**

- Azarel Moros (Desperate for help, but suspicious of newcomers. If the party offers genuine aid or knowledge, he warms quickly. He is afraid the mind flayer is hunting him.)

**Monsters:**

- Raveling Wraith -- A translucent, shrieking spirit, shrouded in torn pieces of reality itself. It flickers in and out of view, trailing motes of magical detritus.

*Connected to: Within area: AQ02, AQ03; The Netherwoven Warrens / AO03*

**DM Guidance:**

Azarel Moros is key to understanding the Unraveling Wards. Play up the area's unstable magic: describe shifting architecture, flickering sensation, and sudden DC increases for spells. The Raveling Wraiths are attracted to magical activity and will appear if the PCs use more than two spells or magical items in a short period. The crossroads is an excellent place for the party to learn about the district's dangers and receive guidance from Azarel. If the PCs stabilize the plinth (DC 17 Arcana check), magical dangers lessen in this room.

### AQ02 -- Specter's Choice Alcove

A narrow, shadow-choked alcove where the city's walls seem to bleed into one another. The air here feels dense and echoing, and faint, whispering voices drift from the cracked stones. Motes of silvery mist swirl in slow eddies, and the remnants of furniture--half-melted and fused to the walls--hint at a violent magical upheaval. The alcove is known for presenting visitors with illusory visions, tempting them toward one of several false exits.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: Accessible from Veilwoven Crossroads, though the exit is hard to discern. The room's illusions confuse and delay most travelers.*

>Within the Specter's Choice Alcove, the party meets Mira the Shardseer, who grants cryptic warnings and insight. This chamber tests the party's ability to reason and resist deception.

**Features:**

- **Interesting Architecture**: Walls and ceiling appear to melt and reform, creating different possible layouts with each glance.
- **Echoing Voices**: Whispers drift from the walls, echoing the party's deepest doubts and hopes.
- **Fused Furnishings**: Remnants of tables and chairs are melded into stone, half-subsumed by the walls.

**DC Checks:**

- Investigation DC 15: Discern the real exit from the three illusory ones.
- Insight DC 13: Sense Mira's warnings and interpret her riddles.
- Arcana DC 14: Analyze the magical residue creating the false doors.

**Plot Hooks:**

- Mira the Shardseer offers a vision of the Mind Flayer antagonist, warning that the creature is manipulating reality from the shadows.
- Visions hint at the 'Fractured Memory Niche' as a place where lost truths can be reclaimed.
- If the party treats Mira kindly, she can lead them safely through the most dangerous illusions, once.

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 16 | Disable DC 16 | Trigger DC 14 | Damage: 4d6 psychic damage and the victim is frightened until the end of their next turn.

**Doors:**

- Phantom Exit -- Appears as a shimmering veil at the far end of the alcove. It leads nowhere, vanishing if approached without guidance. -- Trapped

**Loot:**

- Shard of Stabilized Reality (can open the 'East Spiral Door' in Veilwoven Crossroads)
- Spectral Lens (advantage on Perception checks to discern illusions in the Unraveling Districts for 1 hour)
- Small pouch of ancient, silver coins

**Encounters:**

- *roleplay*: Mira the Shardseer offers visions and advice to the party.
- *combat*: An Illusory Specter attacks if the party is disrespectful or attempts to loot the alcove.

**NPCs:**

- Mira the Shardseer (Guarded but helpful to respectful parties. She warns against trusting 'what you wish to see.')

**Monsters:**

- Illusory Specters -- Semi-corporeal spirits that phase in and out of existence, attacking those who disrespect the alcove or attempt to force their way past the illusions.

*Connected to: Within area: AQ01*

**DM Guidance:**

Mira the Shardseer is an important source of information. Play up her fractured, prophetic speech, and give cryptic clues about both the district and the underlying plot. If the party attacks or mistreats her, the Illusory Specter appears immediately. Otherwise, the specter only attacks if they attempt to loot or vandalize the alcove. The party must distinguish real from false exits to escape; failure leads to psychic backlash (see traps).

### AQ03 -- Fractured Memory Niche

A cramped, labyrinthine chamber where the walls are studded with floating fragments of shattered mirrors and memory crystals. Each surface reflects not just the party's faces, but flickers of their past deeds and lost secrets. The air is heavy and cold, laced with the echo of forgotten voices. The center of the room features a cracked pedestal containing a crystalline sphere, its interior swirling with shifting images.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: Accessible from Veilwoven Crossroads, but the shifting mirrors and psychic echoes can confuse unprepared intruders.*

>In the Fractured Memory Niche, the party faces their own past and must win over Raxil the Doubter to gain vital knowledge of the district's secrets and possible ways deeper into the city.

**Features:**

- **Signs Of Age**: Dusty, cracked mirrors and faded crystalline surfaces line the walls.
- **Memory Crystals**: Glowing fragments hover in the air, each pulsing faintly with stored emotion.
- **Crystalline Sphere**: A large, cracked sphere sits on a pedestal, its contents churning with living memories.

**DC Checks:**

- Insight DC 15: Convince Raxil of the party's honest intentions.
- Perception DC 14: Detect the faint outline of the hidden memory panel.
- Arcana DC 16: Analyze the memory crystals for clues about the area's history.

**Plot Hooks:**

- Raxil the Doubter provides information about a hidden passage to the Subreality Undercity, but only if the party can earn his trust.
- The Journal Fragment hints at the location of Xhalruun the Unraveled's lair.
- The crystalline sphere shows glimpses of the Mind Flayer antagonist manipulating events from behind the scenes.

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 17 | Disable DC 19 | Trigger DC 15 | Damage: 5d6 psychic damage and disadvantage on Insight and Investigation checks in the area for 1 hour.

**Doors:**

- Hidden Memory Panel -- A mirrored panel set into the north wall, visible only to those who have proven themselves to Raxil. -- Trapped

**Loot:**

- Memory Crystal (stores a single truthful memory, can be used to resist mind-altering effects once)
- Journal Fragment (contains cryptic notes about a secret entrance to the Subreality Undercity)
- Crystal Sphere of the Niche (focus: once per day, advantage on Insight checks)

**Encounters:**

- *roleplay*: Raxil the Doubter interrogates the party, challenging their memories and intentions.
- *combat*: A Mirrorborn Echo attacks if the party mishandles the memory crystals or tries to steal from the niche.

**NPCs:**

- Raxil the Doubter (Deeply suspicious. Raxil will quiz the party about their motives and will only share knowledge if convinced of their sincerity.)

**Monsters:**

- Mirrorborn Echo -- A shimmering doppelganger formed from the party's own memories and regrets, attacking with psychic projections.

*Connected to: Within area: AQ01*

**DM Guidance:**

Raxil the Doubter is paranoid and will not cooperate unless the party passes a social challenge (Insight DC 15 or a compelling argument). The Mirrorborn Echo appears if the party tampers with the memory crystals or tries to take the crystalline sphere without Raxil's blessing. This chamber is ideal for character development, as reflections show the party's failures and triumphs.
