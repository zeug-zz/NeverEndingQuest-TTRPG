# Into the Deepvault

## Introduction

### Module Overview
Beneath the stone-browed lands of Deepvault Hold, this adventure unfurls as a winding descent through haunted vaultlands, ancestral catacombs, rune-scarred wastes, and the shuddering depths below, where vanished miners, restless dead, and rival seekers all circle the same buried catastrophe. The party begins amid mist, rumor, and tremor-wrought dread, then follows a chain of ancient seals and waking powers toward a final reckoning with the thing stirring under the world. Along the way, the module presents 10 NPCs to bargain with or beware, 20 plot points to drive the journey, 5 distinct locations to explore, and 14 creature stat blocks to hurl into the dark, all braided together in a tale of treasure, terror, and the echoing price of disturbing what was meant to sleep forever.

### The Story So Far
The story opens in the Shadowed Vaultlands, where old dwarven legends cling to the mist like cobwebs and the earth itself has begun to groan with buried unrest. Miners vanish, explorers do not return, and the party is drawn toward a sealed portal hidden in a sunken plaza, where ancestral wards and prowling shadows guard the first step into the forgotten depths. What begins as a hunt for lost treasure soon reveals a deeper wound: the vaults are not merely abandoned, but bound to a chain of disturbances spreading far beyond their cracked stones.

As the party descends, the trouble widens into the Vaulted Marches, then into Moorhollow's Ancestral Catacombs, where the dead whisper of broken oaths and old grievances. From there the trail drives onward to the Forgebound Wastes, where magical storms rage over fused stone and molten veins, and a Heart Crucible pulses with the heat of some ancient design. Each region reveals another shard of the same truth: factions, spirits, relics, and guardians are all reacting to a vast awakening beneath Deepvault, and every choice the party makes either steadies the land or tears it further apart.

At last, the path leads to Grayspire's End and the Sundered Depths, where duergar rune-warriors carve their blasphemous sigils around a colossal, cracked vault. There, under the command of Varkhaz the Stonebound, the final ritual strains against the last seals of an ancient horror. The adventure builds toward a desperate confrontation in which the party must stop the awakening, survive the collapse, and decide whether Deepvault Hold is saved, scarred, or swallowed by the darkness below.

### Running the Adventure
This adventure is designed for a party of 3rd--5th level characters and works best with a group of four to five adventurers, though it can be adjusted for smaller or larger tables by trimming enemy numbers or strengthening key foes. Before play, the Dungeon Master should review the regional connections between the five locations, note which NPCs can serve as guides, rivals, or warning voices, and prepare for a steady escalation from investigation to delving, negotiation, and hard-won battle. A little forethought with travel, faction motives, and the recurring signs of subterranean unrest will help the whole module feel like one long, ominous heartbeat beneath the world.

# Plot Overview

Beneath the mossy stones and moonlit moors of Deepvault Hold, something ancient is waking--and it answers to no living name. When tremors swallow miners whole, shadows stalk forgotten vaults, and the dead begin whispering through ancestral halls, a band of adventurers is drawn into a chain of buried secrets that winds from the mist-choked Vaultlands to the fire-lit Forgebound Wastes and down, ever down, into the Sundered Depths where a rune-forged conspiracy claws at the heart of the world. Treasures lie sealed behind dwarven wards, spirits brood over broken oaths, relics hum with forbidden power, and rival factions all hunger for the truth, but every descent reveals a darker mystery: these scattered hauntings are not separate calamities, but the trembling echoes of one vast, slumbering terror beneath Deepvault's bones. As the stones crack and the old powers stir, the party must choose whether to appease the dead, outwit the living, or plunge into the abyss itself before the whole realm is remade in darkness.

### PP001 -- Into the Shadowed Vaultlands

The party is drawn to the Shadowed Vaultlands, where legends speak of ancient dwarven treasures and recent tremors have caused miners and explorers to vanish. Locals warn of prowling shadows and ancestral wards. Navigating the mist-shrouded maze, the party must find the sealed entrance to the vaults, setting in motion the events that will echo throughout all of Deepvault Hold's domains.

### PP002 -- The Vault's Hidden Gate

Within a sunken stone plaza, the party discovers the sealed entrance to the underground vaults--a moss-encrusted portal locked by a dwarven ritual. To open the gate and access the true depths, they must solve a puzzle using clues from the environment and local legends, all while fending off aggressive creatures drawn to the area's lingering magic. Rival explorers, sensing an opportunity, might force a tense negotiation or confrontation.

### PP003 -- Descent Beneath the Stones

With the portal opened, the party descends into the ancient vaults, encountering crumbling corridors and awakened guardians. Echoes of past tragedies linger, and as the party delves deeper, they uncover hints of disturbances radiating beyond the Vaultlands--signs that something stirs in the wider Deepvault.

### PP004 -- The Vault's Heart

In the deepest chamber, the party confronts the spirit-warden and its monstrous creations. To claim the legendary treasure and uncover the truth behind the disturbances, the party must destroy or appease the spirit. Their actions here send ripples through the vaults and awaken echoes that reach the surface, drawing the attention of factions in the Vaulted Marches.

### PP005 -- Whispers Beneath the Marches

News of the disturbances draws the party to the Vaulted Marches, where cracked stone arches and misty moors are alive with rumors of old powers awakening. Factions vie for control, and local guides speak of echoing voices at night and glimpses of torchlight vanishing into the ground. The party must decide how to investigate and whom to trust, as the unrest from the Vaultlands begins to spread.

### PP006 -- Descent to Hidden Vaults

Guided by scavenger maps, ghostly whispers, or rival treasure hunters, the party locates a viable entry into the tangled vaults beneath the Marches. As they explore, they encounter direct opposition from other vault-seekers and must choose between confrontation, negotiation, or stealth. The echoes from the Vaultlands hint that something deeper has awakened, urging the party onward.

### PP007 -- The Heart of the Vaulted Marches

Deep within the Marches, the party finds the source of the disturbances--a powerful relic or slumbering entity bound beneath the stones, now stirring due to increasing intrusion. The vault's defenses are failing, and competing factions converge. The party must choose their approach, and the consequences of their actions begin to affect the entire region.

### PP008 -- Echoes Resolved

In a climactic confrontation, the powers awoken in the Marches must be dealt with--whether ancient guardian, vengeful spirit, or the unleashed relic itself. The party's choices determine whether the Marches are cleansed, cursed anew, or claimed by a new power. The consequences ripple outward, their fame (or infamy) spreading among explorers and spirits alike.

### PP009 -- Shadows Beneath the Yew: Entering the Ancestral Catacombs

With unrest radiating from the vaults, the party is beckoned to Moorhollow and its Ancestral Catacombs. The recent surge of undead and angry spirits is now understood as part of the same disturbance. The villagers beg the party to descend into the necropolis, discover the cause of the unrest, and restore peace before the corruption spreads further.

### PP010 -- The Whispering Maze: Navigating the Catacombs

Within the catacombs, the party must traverse twisting stone corridors haunted by illusory echoes and spectral traps. The dead stir at every misstep, and the tunnels themselves seem to whisper of the greater unrest emanating from the Depths below.

### PP011 -- Legacy of Wrath: The Hall of Ancestors

The party discovers the Hall of Ancestors, its spirits restless from a broken oath or desecration. Parley and reparations may avert violence, or the party may be forced to fight the vengeful dead. The origins of these troubles are traced to the deeper disturbances in the Forgebound Wastes and Sundered Depths.

### PP012 -- Into the Deepvault: Confronting the Catacomb's Wrath

At the catacombs' heart, the party faces the vengeful Lord or Lady of the Dead--an ancestral spirit twisted by the same forces threatening the surface. Only by resolving the ancient grievance or defeating the corrupted spirit can the party bring peace to the catacombs and stem the spread of darkness to the Forgebound Wastes.

### PP013 -- Arrival on the Forgebound Wastes

With rumors of magical storms and relics stirring, the party travels to the Forgebound Wastes. Here, the disturbance beneath Deepvault has twisted land and spirit alike. Warring scavenger bands and animated relics block the way to the Heart Crucible, an artifact whose power may be key to quelling the unrest or unleashing it further.

### PP014 -- Through the Thrumming Plains

Within the Wastes' interior, the party traverses fused stone and bone, alive with unpredictable magic. They must decipher runic patterns and survive environmental hazards, encountering outcast mages and spirits who reveal that the unrest is linked to the rune-forge deep below.

### PP015 -- Crossing the Molten Veins

The party must navigate rivers of liquid metal and crumbling causeways, guarded by warped constructs and living relics. Choices here--stealth, brute force, or negotiation--determine their strength as they approach the Crucible and the runes that link all of Deepvault's unrest.

### PP016 -- The Crucible's Heart

At the wastes' fiery core, the party discovers the Heart Crucible--an ancient forge-throne entwined with colossal runes and the remnants of stone-bound spirits. A final confrontation unfolds: a transformed relic-keeper attempts to consume the Crucible's power to reshape the wastes, linking the unrest to the Sundered Depths below.

### PP017 -- Descent into the Sundered Depths

Lady Brunna Ironsong summons the party to Grayspire's End, above a yawning fissure--the only known entrance to the Sundered Depths, where seismic tremors and missing miners point to a final, world-threatening awakening. The party must descend, uncover the source, and rescue any survivors. The journey draws together the threads from all previous regions.

### PP018 -- Labyrinth of Shadows

Within the Depths, the party must navigate shattered corridors, evading deadly chasms and unstable rockfalls. They discover that Duergar Rune-Warriors, led by Varkhaz the Stonebound, are carving runes to awaken something ancient--runic energies that connect to the disturbances in all previous areas.

### PP019 -- The Shattered Vault

The party penetrates deeper, finding the Duergar ritual site: a colossal stone vault cracked wide by recent quakes. Here, the Duergar channel rune-magic to awaken an ancient, slumbering horror. The party must confront Varkhaz the Stonebound and his rune-warriors in a desperate battle amid shifting rock and magical surges.

### PP020 -- Into the Deepvault: The Abyss Stirs

With the ritual interrupted or fulfilled, the Depths begin to collapse as the ancient horror either wakes or remains sealed. The party must escape, rescue survivors, and confront the consequences of their actions. The fate of Deepvault Hold--and the world above--rests on the party's choices, with their deeds echoing in legend.

# Locations

## DA001 -- The Shadowed Vaultlands

Low mist clings to the Shadowed Vaultlands, turning the broken stone outcroppings and half-sunken ruins into a dreamlike maze where every shape seems older than memory. The air is cool, damp, and unnaturally hushed, so that even a careless step feels loud against the soft drip of hidden water and the whisper of moss underfoot. Faded dwarven carving marks the surviving stonework, hinting that this place was once part of a proud and carefully ordered complex before time, collapse, and secrecy buried it beneath earth and legend. For a Dungeon Master, the area feels like a threshold between the living world and something long entombed, a place where treasure hunters, grave robbers, and the merely curious all risk becoming another forgotten layer in the vaultlands' history.

The Vaultlands serve as an outer shell to the deeper mystery of the adventure, guiding intruders from weathered ruins into the more sacred and perilous depths below. Its chambers connect the story by revealing the dwarves' lost craftsmanship, the signs of hasty arcane work, and the first hints that the site was not merely abandoned but actively sealed, studied, or defended. The route onward leads into the Ancestral Catacombs, making this region both a warning and a prologue: the surface ruins test the party's caution before the true secrets of the dead are reached. Within the gloom, giant wolf spiders lurk in the quieter chambers, while animated tools still obey the broken commands of a long-vanished runesmith, suggesting that the vaults remain half-alive with old purpose. These guardians, along with the lingering traces of dwarven lore and experimental magic, make the Shadowed Vaultlands feel like a place where every corridor is an echo of some larger, buried plot, and where each passage draws the party closer to the catacombs and the heart of the adventure.

**Area Type:** Dungeon | **Recommended Level:** 3

An imposing stone arch rises from the misty ground, carved with faded dwarven runes. Moss and lichen cling to its foundations, and the flagstones beneath are slick with dew. The tunnel beyond slopes gently down into the earth, its walls echoing faintly with the sound of dripping water. A subtle draft hints at deeper chambers below.

**Doors:**

## HA003 -- The Ancestral Catacombs

Beneath the leaning mausoleums and black yew roots, the Ancestral Catacombs brood like a wound in the earth, all damp stone, funereal silence, and whispering dead. The air here tastes of dust, iron, and old incense, while pale runes flicker along sarcophagi and ironwork as if the tomb itself still remembers the names of those interred within. What makes this place distinctive is the sense that it is not merely a burial complex, but a sealed legacy of dwarven craft and funerary magic gone sour: every corridor seems designed to honor the dead, yet every shadow feels ready to rise in accusation. The spirits that haunt the halls do not drift aimlessly; they linger with purpose, guarding secrets, punishing trespass, and reacting with fury to any disturbance of the catacombs' sacred order.

The catacombs' chambers tie directly into the adventure's central mystery of corrupted burial rites and forgotten experiments. One wing preserves relics of alchemical practice, where rune-etched benches and shattered vessels hint that the dead were once used in dangerous rites, and where a restless alchemical wraith embodies the lingering consequences of that work. Deeper within, iron-barred soul cells hold the clearest evidence that something was imprisoned here--perhaps miners, perhaps spirits, perhaps both--and the trapped miner Derval offers a living witness to the catacombs' recent horrors, while ironbound guardians enforce the old warding protocols with merciless discipline. Elsewhere, a shattered laboratory and its fused instruments suggest a catastrophic breach between craft and necromancy, animated now by a shatterglass amalgam that turns the site's own broken experiments into a threat. Together, these spaces create a progression from investigation to revelation to confrontation, each room exposing another layer of the catacombs' desecrated past.

This area serves as a hinge between the broader adventure locations, opening into the Shadowed Vaultlands and the Vaulted Marches and being reachable from both. That makes the catacombs an ideal crossroads for backtracking, secret passages, and revelations that recontextualize earlier discoveries elsewhere in the dungeon. The DM can use the connecting routes to let clues recovered in the catacombs point outward toward hidden vaults or marching grounds, while threats from those regions can seep back in through breaches, patrols, or restless dead. ## OV002 -- The Vaulted Marches

The Vaulted Marches feel like a borderland caught between burial and ruin, where the land itself seems to have forgotten whether it is a road, a graveyard, or the roof of some vast forgotten underworld. Cracked arches jut from the mists like broken ribs, half-buried vault mouths gape among thorn and heather, and every step carries the uneasy sense that the ground has settled over something far older than the travelers walking it. The place is distinctive for its layered silence: wind sighs through stonework, echoes arrive a heartbeat too late, and the occasional flicker of distant torchlight suggests that the marches are not empty at all, but merely patient. Treasure hunters prowl here alongside things that should never have learned to hunger in the dark, making the area feel both opportunistic and haunted.

This region serves as a liminal hinge in the adventure, a place where the story's threads of ancestral ruin and industrial corruption brush against one another. Its chambers and vault fragments do not stand alone; they funnel explorers toward the deeper mysteries of the Ancestral Catacombs and the harsh, fire-scarred Forgebound Wastes, while also allowing passage back from those places into this mist-choked threshold. The ruins here hint at the larger plot through scattered dwarven craftsmanship, broken reliquaries, and sealed passages that suggest something was hidden, protected, or stolen long ago. Each space reinforces that sense of connection: a crossing of toppled pillars opens toward older burial ways, while smaller vaults and collapsed niches feel like spillways from a greater network of tombs and forge-ruins beyond.

The inhabitants of the Marches reflect that uneasy overlap between guardian, scavenger, and infestation. **Area Type:** Mixed | **Recommended Level:** 3

## RF004 -- The Forgebound Wastes

The Forgebound Wastes seethe like a wound that never quite closes: a blasted expanse of slag-fields and molten channels where the air itself shivers with heat and old enchantment. Runes spiral through the ash in ghostly loops, and every surface bears the scars of a catastrophe that did not merely destroy the forge-work here, but remade it into something half-alive. Stone has fused with bone, iron with glass, and the ground thrums beneath every step as though some buried engine still labors in fury. This is a place of harsh beauty and constant threat, where the glow of metal rivers paints the darkness in orange and gold, and where even silence feels hammered thin by ancient power.

The area's chambers feel like broken organs of a once-great industrial heart, each one preserving a different fragment of the adventure's central mystery: a defensive post twisted by magical backlash, a smith's private refuge warped into a reliquary of failed craft, and a shattered crossing that serves as the broken hinge between deeper routes. These spaces do not merely offer combat encounters; they reveal what happened here, how the forge's defenders fell, and why the eldritch energies still cling so fiercely to the site. Clues found among scorched tools, fused stonework, and rune-scarred masonry can point the party toward the larger secrets of the adventure, while the layout itself encourages movement between the Vaulted Marches and the Sundered Depths, making the Wastes feel like a crucible where paths intersect and old grudges still burn.

The inhabitants are as warped as the land. Molten Bone Golems lumber through the broken intersections like living siege engines, their frames glowing from within as if the dead still feed the fire. Ashen Wraiths haunt the ruined watchpost, remnants of sentries or victims bound to the place by smoke and unfinished duty, while a Forged Sentry guards the smith's refuge with tireless, ritual precision, as though its maker's last command never ended. Together, these foes suggest a complex history of guardianship, betrayal, and magical contamination, and their presence ties the area tightly to the broader dungeon network: one route opens back toward the Vaulted Marches, another descends toward the Sundered Depths, and both directions make the Forgebound Wastes feel like a perilous crossroads where the adventure's deeper truths are forged in fire.

**Area Type:** Mixed | **Recommended Level:** 4

## SD005 -- The Sundered Depths

Beneath the shattered earth, the Sundered Depths brood like a wound that never quite closes. This dungeon is all broken stone, mineral stink, and black gulfs that swallow torchlight whole, where every footfall sends pebbles skittering into unseen distances and every distant cry seems to come from the rock itself. Once-proud dwarven craftsmanship survives here only in torn banners, soot-stained carvings, and half-buried mechanisms, all of it warped by cataclysm and age. The place feels ancient and unstable, a maze of collapsed caverns and knife-edged ledges that constantly reminds the party that the ground beneath them is as dangerous as anything that hunts in the dark.

The Sundered Depths serve as a buried hinge in the adventure's larger shape, linking the ruined underworld to the Forgebound Wastes beyond. Its chambers are not merely obstacles but clues: a shattered passage bears the scars of old transit, a ruined forge hints at the industry that once fed the region's greater machines, and a fractured audience hall suggests a civilization that fell before the present crisis began. The dungeon's layout encourages the Dungeon Master to reveal the story in layers, with each unstable chamber exposing more of the disaster that split this place apart and the forces now stirring within it. The route through these depths naturally feeds into the next region, making the dungeon feel like a descent toward the source of the adventure's wider corruption rather than an isolated detour.

What dwells here underscores that sense of a place half-dead and half-awake. Chasm lurkers prowl the broken runways and fissures, waiting to drag the unwary into the dark, while animated forge-tools clatter to life around the ancient workshop, as if some lingering command still drives them to impossible labor. In the echoing hall, a wrathful echo wraith haunts the memory of lost grandeur, its voice carrying the grief of the dead through the stone. Together, these threats give the Dungeon Master a spectrum of menace--from predatory ambushes to uncanny guardians to a spectral remnant of the past--while the dungeon's connections back to the Forgebound Wastes make it clear that whatever power stirs there has already left its mark deep below.

**Area Type:** Dungeon | **Recommended Level:** 5
