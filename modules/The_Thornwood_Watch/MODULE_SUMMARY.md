# The Thornwood Watch

## Introduction

### Module Overview
In this shadow-soaked adventure by MoonlightByte, the party is drawn into a desperate hunt across a wounded frontier where caravans vanish, bandits kneel to a stranger's will, and an ancient corruption stirs beneath the roots of the Thornwood. The module opens at Rangers' Outpost in the grip of panic, where Commander Elen seeks aid against a ruthless enemy whose trail leads from a massacre-strewn road to a crumbling watchtower, then onward to a blighted grove and the hidden ritual site of Malarok the Corruptor. Along the way, the Dungeon Master will weave together 8 NPCs, 5 plot points, 3 evocative locations, and 26 creature stat blocks into a tale of pursuit, rescue, and occult ruin, all tuned for heroes of levels 3--5.

### The Story So Far
The adventure begins in grim urgency as the party arrives at Rangers' Outpost and finds the frontier reeling from a string of slaughtered caravans, each attack executed with eerie precision. Commander Elen presses the heroes into service, and the captured bandit leader Rusk trembles before a master he dares not name. Through interrogation and hard-won truth, the party learns that the bandits lair in an old watchtower deep in Thornwood, and that their new patron is Malarok, a sorcerer whose shadow reaches farther than any common outlaw's. From that revelation, the hunt is set in motion, and the wilds themselves seem to bristle in answer.

The trail winds through the Thornwood Wilds, where hostile beasts and roaming bandits guard the path to the ruined tower, and where surviving captives may yet be found clinging to hope. Clues gathered there point north to a corrupted grove, a place where the forest has been twisted by dark magic and the air itself feels bruised by cold. There, the party follows the chill of a strange iron charm and the signs of a power source older and fouler than the bandit raids, until the path leads to a hidden ruin where Malarok performs a vile rite over a pulsating Voidstone Shard. In the final confrontation, the heroes must break his guard, end his ritual, and shatter the stone's corruption before it can spread further--only to return and discover that the story is not yet done, for Elen has vanished in response to a new and urgent mystery in Harrow's Hollow.

### Running the Adventure
This adventure is designed for a party of 3--5 characters, ideally beginning at level 3 and reaching level 5 by the end, and it rewards steady investigation, wilderness travel, and a climactic magical showdown. Before play, the DM should review the three locations, note the timing of Elen's departure after Malarok falls, and prepare to pivot smoothly from this module's resolution into the next hook; a few spare names, battle maps or sketches for the watchtower and grove, and a clear sense of the bandit-to-sorcerer chain of command will help the tale unfold with ominous momentum.

# Plot Overview

When the party reaches Rangers' Outpost, they find not a haven but a wound in the wilds: caravans have been butchered with chilling precision, the woods are stirring with unnatural rage, and fear hangs over the camp like smoke. Commander Elen turns to the strangers at her gate with a grim bargain, for the only witness is a captured bandit chief who cowers before a new and unseen master--a sorcerer named Malarok, whose shadow stretches from a crumbling watchtower in the Thornwood to a blighted grove where the trees themselves seem poisoned by dark magic. As the party follows the trail deeper into the forest, they uncover signs of a ritual power far older and more terrible than banditry, one that pulses from a Voidstone shard and calls to corruption, blood, and ruin. But even if they can break the sorcerer's hold and shatter the stone, the victory may only open a darker road ahead, for in the aftermath of the battle, Elen herself vanishes into a new mystery, leaving behind a desperate message and a haunting invitation to follow the trail to Harrow's Hollow.

### PP001 -- Desperate Defense

The party arrives at Rangers' Outpost to find it in crisis. Three caravans have been massacred in five days with unnatural precision. Ranger Elen, the outpost's commander, recruits the party. Her prime lead is the captured Bandit Leader, Rusk, a man now terrified of his new master. The party must interrogate Rusk to learn the location of the main bandit camp and the identity of the sorcerer, Malarok, who now commands them. IMPORTANT: This plot point should be marked COMPLETED immediately after Rusk reveals the bandit camp location (old watchtower in Thornwood) and Malarok's identity - do not wait for expedition planning or other preparations.

### PP002 -- Into the Thornwood

Following leads from Rusk, the party ventures into the Thornwood Wilds to find the main bandit camp, located in an old, crumbling watchtower. They must overcome unnaturally aggressive wildlife and bandit patrols to reach the camp, rescue any surviving captives, and find clues about Malarok's ultimate goal.

### PP003 -- The Corrupted Grove

The trail leads north to a blighted part of the forest where the trees are twisted and the air hums with dark energy. The party must navigate this dangerous grove to find the source of the sorcerer's power, following the increasing cold of the iron charm from Maelo.

### PP004 -- Confronting the Corruptor

Deep within the ruins, the party finds Malarok the Corruptor. He is performing a dark ritual to absorb the power of a large, pulsating Voidstone Shard set upon an altar. He is a formidable spellcaster, protected by elite bodyguards and the stone's foul energy. The party must defeat Malarok and destroy the Voidstone to end his threat. The purified water in the Rune-etched Ritual Vial from Maelo may be crucial for cleansing the Voidstone's corruption. SPECIAL DM INSTRUCTION: When completing this plot point after Malarok's defeat, have Scout Elen suddenly receive an urgent magical message. She should refuse to join the party back to the outpost, explaining she must leave immediately for Harrow's Hollow where Scout Elen has gone missing. Have her say she'll meet Eirik there if he follows. Remove Elen from all location NPCs in this module.

### PP005 -- Report to Commander Elen (but she's gone!)

*Prerequisites: PP001, PP002, PP003, PP004*

When the party returns to Rangers' Outpost to report their success, they discover Commander Elen has vanished! Ranger Marcus explains that Elen received an urgent message about Scout Elen's disappearance in Harrow's Hollow and departed immediately, leaving only a hastily scrawled note: 'Scout Elen missing in Harrow's Hollow - same signs as here. Must go now. Tell Eirik to follow if he can. -E'. Ranger Marcus gives the party their rewards but is clearly worried about Elen. DM NOTE: This is a plot hook to the Keep of Doom module. After giving rewards and wrapping up this module's storylines, inform players that they can travel to Harrow's Hollow to find Elen and investigate the mysterious disappearances.
