# The Hidden City of Numillian - Module Summary

## Overview
A hidden elven city tests intruders with riddles and memory magic, while flooding wards and rival factions threaten to expose a vault of forbidden power.

## Module Conflicts
- **Wardkeepers vs. Vault-Seekers** (local): Within Numillian, the wardkeepers who maintain the city's illusions and memory seals are locked in a covert struggle with a faction of scholars and dissidents who believe the hidden vault should be opened, studied, or relocated before its magic becomes too dangerous to contain.
- **Floodgate Sabotage** (local): A series of deliberate tamperings with the city's flood-control wards has caused dangerous backflow in lower districts, threatening homes, archives, and sacred chambers while also masking the movements of hidden agents.
- **Surface Envoy Tensions** (regional): A nearby regional power has sent envoys, spies, and magical surveyors to uncover Numillian's location, forcing the city to balance secrecy against the need for trade, diplomacy, and outside assistance.
