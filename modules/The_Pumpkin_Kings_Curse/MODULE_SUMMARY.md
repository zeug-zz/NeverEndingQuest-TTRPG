# The Pumpkin Kings Curse

## Introduction

### Module Overview
This adventure is a lantern-lit tale of harvest cheer curdled into dread, set in the fertile but haunted lands around Greenfields Vale, where a jubilant festival hides a curse that has begun to rot the roots of the countryside. The party arrives amid song, cider, and scarecrow shadows, only to find nightmares stalking children, blighted fields whispering at dusk, and a mayor whose welcome is warm but whose answers are not. Across the course of the module, the Dungeon Master will guide the players through 17 NPCs, 7 plot points, 6 evocative locations, and 22 creature stat blocks, weaving a mystery of rural superstition, restless dead, and a sovereign of autumnal malice whose shadow lengthens over every pumpkin patch and grave.

### The Story So Far
Greenfields Vale basks beneath the bright banners of the harvest revels when the party first arrives, but the merriment is already fraying at the edges. Rumors drift through the crowd like chimney smoke: shadowy figures moving among the corn, strange tokens left at crossroads, and children waking in terror from dreams of a crowned figure in the dark. As the party questions villagers and earns trust--or suspicion--they begin to see that the festival is not merely a celebration, but a fragile mask stretched over a spreading blight.

Their investigation leads beyond the village green and into the fields where the land itself seems to remember old wrongs. At the Corn Maze of Screams, ghostly signs and desperate survivors point toward a curse that reaches into the Wailing Cornfields and the Fields of Supplication, where ancient powers still cling to the soil. There, the party seeks the Ember Gourd, a sacred relic needed to challenge the force behind the haunting, and the trail of misery leads onward to the Old Graveyard, where the dead do not rest easily and the Noose Wraith guards the truth of the Pumpkin King's rise.

At last, the journey turns toward the Circle of Stalks, the Pumpkin King's final stronghold, where the harvest court gathers beneath a sky heavy with omen. Allies, blessings, and hard-won knowledge all converge for the final reckoning, and the party must use the Ember Gourd and every boon they have earned to shatter the curse at its source. If they prevail, the land is freed from its spectral tithe; if they fail, the harvest's shadow spreads beyond the Vale and into the wider world.

### Running the Adventure
This adventure is best suited for a party of 4--5 characters at levels 3--5, though a strong or cautious group can be adjusted with care. Before play, the Dungeon Master should read ahead through the investigation chain, note which NPCs can reveal which clues, and prepare the atmosphere of each location so the festival's brightness can slowly sour into dread; a few handouts, eerie harvest music, and a clear sense of the curse's escalation will help the tale bloom from rustic mystery into a final, candle-flaring confrontation.

# Plot Overview

As the lanterns of Greenfields Vale glow gold beneath a jubilant harvest moon, strangers arrive to a village dressed in laughter that cannot quite hide its fear: children wake screaming from the same nightmare, crops blacken at the roots, and shadowy shapes are said to wander the furrows after dusk. Welcomed by Mayor Tilda Bramble's careful smiles and the uneasy murmurs of the folk, the adventurers are drawn into a mystery that stretches from festive greens to haunted cornfields, from whispering crossroads to a maze where the dead still cry out. What begins as a season of plenty becomes a race against a creeping curse and the ancient bargain behind it, leading toward a sacred relic, a judgment long overdue, and the terrible throne of the Pumpkin King himself--where the fate of the valley, and every soul bound to its harvest, hangs on one final, desperate stand.

### PP001 -- Arrival Amid the Harvest Revels

The party arrives in Greenfields Vale as the annual harvest festival is reaching its peak. Festivities mask a growing unease--rumors circulate of shadowy figures in the fields and children plagued by nightmares. Mayor Tilda Bramble welcomes the adventurers but avoids discussing festival origins. As the party mingles, villagers approach with troubling tales, setting the stage for their investigation. Early choices here build trust or suspicion among the townsfolk, impacting later events across the region.

### PP002 -- Whispers on the Village Outskirts

Investigating the outskirts of Greenfields Vale, the party learns from Guildmaster Fenric Clay, farmhands, and Amanita Gorse about blighted crops and shadowy trespassers. Evidence of ancient pacts and spirit tokens at the crossroads point to a curse affecting not just the Vale, but neighboring lands. This prompts a decision: investigate the creeping omens within the Vale or travel to nearby Tanglewood Verge to follow up on linked disturbances.

### PP003 -- Shadows Over Festival Green

Ominous omens intensify as the Harvest Festival Green becomes a focal point for supernatural disturbances. The party conducts a deeper investigation at the festival grounds, encountering minor hauntings and uncovering evidence that the curse is spreading to the outlying Wailing Cornfields and Fields of Supplication. Villagers recall similar events in neighboring settlements, urging the party to pursue these leads before the curse erupts into catastrophe.

### PP004 -- Into the Corn Maze of Screams

Following supernatural signs and villagers' pleas, the party ventures into the infamous Corn Maze of Screams bordering the Vale and the Wailing Cornfields. Inside, shifting paths and ghostly figures test their resolve. At the maze's heart, they find Widow Grella and her grandson Tom, pursued by a sinister shadow. Surviving the maze's illusions and vengeful spirits, the party learns that the Pumpkin King's annual tithe is imminent--and that the curse touches the Cornfields to the west.

### PP005 -- Seeking the Ember Gourd

Following clues gathered from the cornfields and the testimonies of witnesses, the party ventures into the Fields of Supplication to locate the legendary Ember Gourd. This sacred artifact is essential for breaking the Pumpkin King's curse, but it lies hidden in dangerous territory where malign spirits guard ancient secrets. The party must navigate treacherous ground, solve supernatural puzzles, and prove their worthiness to claim this powerful relic.

### PP006 -- The Gallows Judgment

Armed with the Ember Gourd and the knowledge to use it, the party follows the trail of the curse to its source: the Old Graveyard where executions once took place. Here they confront the Noose Wraith, a powerful manifestation of the injustices that first gave the Pumpkin King his power. Victory against this mini-boss provides crucial knowledge about the Pumpkin King's origins and reveals the location of his final stronghold.

### PP007 -- The Harvest's End

In the climactic final confrontation, the party enters the Circle of Stalks where the Pumpkin King holds court. This epic battle tests everything they have learned, every alliance they have forged, and every magical aid they have gathered. Using the Ember Gourd, Willa's chant, blessed weapons, and their own courage, they must defeat the Pumpkin King and break the curse that has plagued the land. Victory brings peace to the region and freedom to all the trapped souls; failure means the curse spreads beyond the harvest lands.

# Locations

## BOO001 -- Fields of Supplication

The Fields of Supplication sprawl like a half-forgotten prayer laid flat beneath a bruised, unbroken sky. Here, the land itself seems to mourn: meadows grow wild around toppled shrines, muddy paths sink underfoot, and broken lanterns lie scattered like extinguished hopes. The air is heavy with damp earth, old blood, and the faintest trace of incense long since drowned by rain. Every gust carries whispers--snatches of desperate petitions, pleas for mercy, and names that the living no longer remember. Malign spirits haunt the offerings left to rot in the open, and the field's strange stillness is broken only by the creak of rotting wood, the whisper of grain, and the rustle of things that should have stayed buried.

This place serves as a haunted crossroads in the adventure's larger web, drawing the party toward the fate of the vanished and the blight that clings to the land. Its scattered shrines, ritual spaces, and abandoned working yards are not merely scenery: each one reveals a different fragment of the same tragedy, from failed harvest rites to desperate funerary supplications and the violence that followed. The fields connect naturally to the Wailing Cornfields, the Old Graveyard, and the Hollow Field, making them a central passage between the adventure's more cultivated, more funereal, and more desolate regions. A DM can use this area to seed clues, foreshadow deeper corruption, and let the characters feel that every path they take is part of a larger, mournful pattern.

The inhabitants reinforce that sense of sacred ruin. The Spirit of Supplication lingers at the lantern-crossroads like a memory of faith that has outlived its worshippers, while lantern husks, harvest shades, bloodshadows, grain wraiths, and other field-born horrors twist the land's old rituals into predatory forms. A wandering supplicant and the petitioning shades hint at the human stories behind the haunting, while Old Crone Willa and Sybil Nettlemire suggest that not all who remain here are helpless victims. Around them stalk guardians made monstrous by devotion and neglect alike: scarecrow sentinels, rope stranglers, stone guardians, and blights born from hay and grain. Together they make the Fields of Supplication feel like a place where prayer, labor, and death have become indistinguishable, and where every route onward leads deeper into the adventure's cursed heart.

**Area Type:** Mixed | **Recommended Level:** 2

### V01 -- Withered Lantern Crossroads

At the heart of the Fields of Supplication, four ancient footpaths intersect atop a patch of churned mud, scattered with the remains of broken lanterns whose pale embers sometimes gutter to life without warning. Faded sigils of bygone harvests are carved into toppled stones, and offerings--wilted flowers, shriveled gourds, and crude dolls--lie abandoned. The air is thick with the scent of wax and smoke, and ghostly whispers tug at the minds of the living.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: Paths are overgrown but navigable; no physical barriers.*

>The crossroads serves as a nexus of spiritual unrest, offering both danger and opportunities for insight into the curse plaguing the fields.

**Features:**

- **Signs Of Age**: Stone markers and lanterns covered in lichen and moss, bearing inscriptions faded by decades of neglect.
- **Interesting Architecture**: The crossroads is marked by converging low stone walls and sunken lantern pedestals, hinting at ancient ritual design.
- **Dust**: Fine gray dust covers everything, occasionally disturbed by unseen feet.

**DC Checks:**

- Religion DC 12: Identify the correct offering to appease the spirits.
- Investigation DC 10: Follow the tracks leading south and east.
- Perception DC 13: Spot a hidden, unlit lantern with runes inside the mud.

**Plot Hooks:**

- The Spirit asks for help to lay the other lost souls to rest, offering cryptic hints about the true nature of the curse.
- A tangle of footprints in the mud suggests someone fled south recently, pursued by something inhuman.

**Loot:**

- Lantern of Fading Hope (casts dim light in a 20-ft radius, reveals invisible spirits within 10 ft while lit)
- Bundle of Sage Stalks (can be burned to repel minor undead)
- Tarnished Silver Offering Coin (worth 5 gp but valuable for ritual use)

**Encounters:**

- A restless Lantern Husk emerges if offerings are disturbed.
- A group of lost spirits gathers at midnight, seeking to repeat the failed harvest ritual.

**NPCs:**

- Spirit of Supplication (Anxious, hopeful for aid but wary of outsiders disrespecting the rituals.)

**Monsters:**

- Lantern Husk -- A twisted scarecrow animated by lingering magic and the resentment of the forgotten dead; it lashes at intruders with burning, waxy tendrils.

*Connected to: Within area: V02, V03, V12*

**DM Guidance:**

This crossroads is a natural gathering point for spirits and lost travelers. Adjust the number of Lantern Husks for party size. The Spirit of Supplication may offer guidance or warnings, especially to those who make a respectful offering (DC 12 Religion or Insight to interpret rituals). If the party lingers or disturbs the offerings, spectral voices grow hostile.

### V02 -- Graven Scarecrow Hollow

This sunken ring is surrounded by leaning, rotted fence posts from which tattered scarecrows dangle. The ground is churned and spongy, scattered with old bones and bits of ribbon. In the hollow's center stands a scarecrow twice as tall as the rest, its 'face' carved from a hollowed gourd etched with crude runes. The air here is colder, and the boundaries between the living and the dead are thin.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: Fenced ring can be entered easily but the ground is treacherous and muddy.*

>A place of failed protection, this hollow holds clues to the curse and a dangerous guardian for the unwary.

**Features:**

- **Dust**: A layer of black, oily dust covers the straw, carrying a faint scent of decay.
- **Interesting Architecture**: The hollow's ring is lined with rickety posts, some carved with crude faces meant to ward off evil.
- **Signs Of Age**: The scarecrows' clothing is rotted and patched, remnants of decades-old harvest festivals.

**DC Checks:**

- Religion DC 12: Perform the proper supplication to pacify the scarecrow.
- Arcana DC 13: Decipher the runes to learn about Elric's role.
- Perception DC 11: Notice the ground is unstable and may collapse if too many people enter at once.

**Plot Hooks:**

- Scraps of a blood-stained festival banner hint at a forgotten ritual that went wrong here.
- The runes on the central scarecrow reference a name--Elric--suggesting he was involved in binding or angering the spirits.

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 12 | Disable DC 12 | Trigger DC 10 | Damage: 1d6 necrotic (a burst of shadowy thorns erupts from beneath the central scarecrow if the runes are tampered with)

**Loot:**

- Rune-Carved Gourd Mask (wearer gains advantage on saving throws against fear for 1 hour)
- Handful of Silver Bells (worth 10 gp, also tinkle softly near undead)
- Bundle of Withered Wheat (when burned, grants resistance to necrotic damage for 1 turn)

**Encounters:**

- Combat with the Scarecrow Sentinel and field rats if the area is disturbed.
- Visions of children weaving between the scarecrows, re-enacting a doomed festival rite.

**Monsters:**

- Scarecrow Sentinel -- A menacing, runic-etched scarecrow animated by necromantic energy, it attacks anyone who approaches without making a proper offering.
- Swarm of Field Rats -- Drawn by blood and carrion, these oversized rats scuttle from beneath the straw, attacking as a pack.

*Connected to: Within area: V01*

**DM Guidance:**

The Scarecrow Sentinel animates and attacks if approached aggressively or if the central gourd is touched without a DC 12 Religion or Arcana check to perform a supplication (offering of wheat, coin, or blood). The rats emerge if there is combat or loud noise. The runes can be deciphered (DC 13 Arcana) to reveal a cryptic warning and Elric's name.

### V03 -- Harvest Warden's Path

A narrow, winding trail lined by splintered wooden figurines and prayer ribbons faded to white. The path's muddy ruts are flecked with bright red berries and dark stains. Old windchimes tinkle whenever the wind blows, though no breeze stirs. At irregular intervals, toppled shrines with offerings in various states of decay can be found. The oppressive atmosphere grows heavier the further one walks.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: The path is passable but treacherous; stepping off invites danger.*

>This winding path provides both danger and critical information, rewarding respectful explorers with lore and minor boons.

**Features:**

- **Interesting Architecture**: Miniature shrines are interspersed along the narrow path, each with unique carvings and decaying offerings.
- **Signs Of Age**: Wooden figurines are cracked and splintered, their painted faces all but erased.

**DC Checks:**

- Religion DC 11: Learn the proper way to walk the path safely.
- Insight DC 11: Realize the Supplicant can be trusted if given a ritual offering.
- Investigation DC 13: Discover the hidden compartment in a shrine.

**Plot Hooks:**

- The Supplicant recalls seeing Elric flee northward, pursued by something that left strange, clawed footprints.
- One of the toppled shrines holds a hidden compartment containing a page from the original harvest pact.

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 13 | Disable DC 11 | Trigger DC 10 | Damage: 1d4 psychic (if a shrine is disturbed, a spectral wail is triggered, stunning the nearest creature until the end of their next turn if they fail a DC 10 Wisdom save)

**Loot:**

- Prayer Ribbon of Protection (advantage vs. charm effects from undead while worn)
- Red Berries of Remembrance (restores 1d4 HP when eaten, but imposes disadvantage on Wisdom saves until short rest)
- Copper Amulet with cracked moonstone (worth 15 gp to the right collector)

**Encounters:**

- A Harvest Shade materializes if the party disrespects the path or shrines.
- The Wandering Supplicant pleads for release or begs to be left alone, depending on the party's approach.

**NPCs:**

- Wandering Supplicant (Fearful, will offer information in exchange for protection or a ritual item.)

**Monsters:**

- Harvest Shade -- A shadowy specter that stalks those who stray from the path, draining warmth and hope from its victims.

*Connected to: Within area: V01, V04*

**DM Guidance:**

The path is safe only for those who walk respectfully and do not disturb the shrines (DC 11 Religion or Insight to discern this). Straying or showing disrespect summons a Harvest Shade. The Wandering Supplicant can be persuaded to share what they know if offered a ribbon, coin, or berry.

### V04 -- Petitioner's Rest

A collapsed glade ringed by the decaying remains of benches and offering tables. The ground is pitted and soft; old bloodstains are visible even after years of rain. At the center stands a ruined altar of blackened stone, surrounded by mossy skulls and shattered ritual implements. Shadows flicker in the corners, and a sense of grief hangs heavy in the air.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: The glade is open but slick with mud and overgrowth; altar is half-buried and easy to miss at first glance.*

>This site offers a crucial clue to the curse and a dangerous threat for rash adventurers.

**Features:**

- **Signs Of Age**: Altar and benches are crumbling, covered in moss, and marked with faded bloodstains.

**DC Checks:**

- Religion DC 13: Make an offering to appease the Petitioning Shade.
- Investigation DC 12: Find the hidden pact fragment beneath a mossy skull.
- Arcana DC 14: Recognize the altar's lingering curse and avoid triggering it.

**Plot Hooks:**

- The fragment of the scroll here, if pieced together with others, reveals how to break the field's curse.
- The Petitioning Shade hints that Elric was last seen making a desperate bargain at the altar, leading to his disappearance.

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 14 | Disable DC 13 | Trigger DC 12 | Damage: 2d6 necrotic (if the altar is moved without a ritual, black fire erupts and engulfs the intruder)

**Loot:**

- Obsidian Ritual Dagger (counts as a +1 dagger vs. undead, but inflicts 1 psychic damage to the wielder when first drawn)
- Bundle of Rare Moss (can be used in potion brewing, worth 25 gp)
- Fragment of the Original Pact Scroll (contains a vital clue about the Pumpkin King's curse)

**Encounters:**

- A tense negotiation with the Petitioning Shade, who may become hostile if angered.
- A sudden attack by the Bloodshadow if the altar is disturbed without respect.

**NPCs:**

- Petitioning Shade (Resigned but curious, eager to barter for peace but easily angered by disrespect.)

**Monsters:**

- Bloodshadow -- A lingering manifestation of all the sacrifices made here, it attacks anyone who tries to loot or defile the altar.

*Connected to: Within area: V03; The Wailing Cornfields / X01; The Old Graveyard / Z01*

**DM Guidance:**

If the party makes a respectful offering (DC 13 Religion), the Petitioning Shade offers a riddle revealing how to find Elric. Looting the altar without appeasing the spirits triggers an attack from the Bloodshadow. The pact fragment is hidden beneath a skull (DC 12 Investigation to find). On combat: Call createEncounter with Bloodshadow (50 XP). On victory: XP is automatically awarded.

### V12 -- Threshing Floor

A wide, circular space paved with ancient stones, where grain was once separated from chaff during harvest seasons. The stones are now cracked and stained with dark patches that gleam wetly despite the absence of rain. Broken threshing tools lie scattered about--flails, sickles, and winnowing baskets--their handles worn smooth by countless hands. The air carries the phantom scent of wheat dust mixed with something more sinister.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: Open area with clear sight lines, but the uneven stone floor can be treacherous.*

>A place where honest labor once blessed the land, now corrupted by dark rituals and haunted by the memory of better times.

**Features:**

- **Ancient Stones**: Weathered paving stones arranged in a perfect circle, some bearing carved symbols related to the harvest.
- **Scattered Tools**: Broken and rusted farming implements telling the story of countless harvests past.
- **Phantom Grain**: Spectral wheat and chaff that appears and disappears, leaving behind the scent of long-dead harvests.

**DC Checks:**

- History DC 12: Recognize the significance of the threshing floor in harvest traditions.
- Perception DC 13: Notice the runic scythe among the scattered tools.
- Religion DC 11: Understand the proper rituals once performed here.

**Plot Hooks:**

- The Thresher's Ghost mentions seeing strange figures carrying bundles toward the Hanging Loft during the last full moon.
- Among the scattered tools, a peculiar scythe bears runes that match those found elsewhere in the curse-touched areas.

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 13 | Disable DC 15 | Trigger DC 11 | Damage: 1d6 slashing (ancient threshing mechanisms activate if pressure is applied to certain stones, swinging phantom flails)

**Loot:**

- Blessed Sickle (functions as a +1 sickle, effective against plant creatures)
- Bag of Ever-Fresh Grain (provides one day's sustenance when consumed)
- Thresher's Token (carved wooden medallion that grants advantage on Survival checks related to agriculture)

**Encounters:**

- *roleplay*: Conversation with the Thresher's Ghost about proper harvest rituals and the curse's origins.
- *combat*: Grain Wraith attacks if the spectral grain or threshing stones are disturbed.

**NPCs:**

- Thresher's Ghost (Melancholy but helpful; will share knowledge of proper harvest rituals if treated with respect.)

**Monsters:**

- Grain Wraith -- A swirling mass of spectral grain and chaff animated by the anger of spoiled harvests, it attacks by engulfing victims in a choking cloud of phantom wheat.

*Connected to: Within area: V01, V13*

**DM Guidance:**

On entering room: Call createEncounter with 3-5 Skeletons (50 XP each) in waves.

### V13 -- Hanging Loft

A raised wooden platform supported by weathered posts, where harvested grain was once hung to dry. Rope and chain still dangle from the rafters, some holding desiccated bundles of herbs and grain, others swaying empty in the still air. The floorboards creak ominously underfoot, and several have rotted through completely. From this elevated position, one can see across the entire Fields of Supplication, a view both commanding and deeply unsettling.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: Requires climbing a ladder to reach; the platform is partially unstable with several dangerous gaps.*

>A key location where the party can gain essential knowledge about breaking the curse, but only if they approach with respect and prove their worthy intentions.

**Features:**

- **Hanging Bundles**: Desiccated herbs and grain suspended from the rafters, some still potent with magical properties.
- **Rotted Floorboards**: Wooden planks weakened by age and weather, creating treacherous footing.
- **Panoramic View**: A commanding view of the Fields of Supplication, revealing the full scope of the curse's influence.

**DC Checks:**

- Persuasion DC 15: Convince Willa to teach the Ember Gourd chant.
- Insight DC 13: Recognize that Willa is testing the party's character and intentions.
- Perception DC 11: Identify which floorboards are unsafe to step on.

**Plot Hooks:**

- Willa knows the Ember Gourd chant but will only teach it to those who first help her complete a purification ritual.
- She warns that the Stone Trough Altar below holds a dangerous artifact that must be claimed before the next new moon.

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 12 | Disable DC 14 | Trigger DC 10 | Damage: 1d8 bludgeoning (rotted floorboards give way under weight, causing a fall to the ground below)

**Doors:**

- Loft Access Ladder -- A rickety wooden ladder leading up to the hanging loft platform.

**Loot:**

- Bundle of Dried Sage (when burned, provides protection from evil spirits for 10 minutes)
- Willa's Herb Pouch (contains rare reagents worth 30 gp to an alchemist)
- Ancient Harvest Almanac (provides detailed information about lunar cycles and harvest timing)

**Encounters:**

- *roleplay*: Crucial conversation with Old Crone Willa about the Ember Gourd chant and curse-breaking rituals.
- *combat*: Rope Stranglers animate if the party shows hostility or greed.

**NPCs:**

- Old Crone Willa (Suspicious of strangers but willing to help those who prove their good intentions; fiercely protective of the old harvest traditions.)

**Monsters:**

- Rope Strangler -- Animate lengths of hemp rope that hang from the rafters, possessed by the spirits of those who met their end here. They attempt to entangle and strangle intruders.

*Connected to: Within area: V12, V14*

**DM Guidance:**

For Willa's quest: Prompt for Animal Handling DC 10. On success: Teach Ember Gourd chant.

### V14 -- Stone Trough Altar

A massive stone basin carved from a single block of granite sits at the center of this sacred space, its surface worn smooth by centuries of ritual use. The trough is filled with brackish water that never seems to evaporate, and strange symbols are etched around its rim. Three ritual stations surround the altar: a grain offering bowl, a blessed lantern holder, and a consecrated blood basin. Candles of various sizes have been placed around the altar, their wax having dripped into fantastic, otherworldly shapes over the years. The Ember Gourd rests within the trough, pulsing with inner fire.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: Open sacred space, but approaching the altar incorrectly can trigger protective magic.*

>The sacred repository of the Ember Gourd, a key artifact essential for breaking the curse that plagues the land.

**Features:**

- **Stone Trough**: A massive granite basin filled with sacred water that never evaporates or becomes foul. Contains the dormant Ember Gourd.
- **Grain Offering Bowl**: A small stone bowl for placing grain offerings as the first step of the ritual.
- **Blessed Lantern Holder**: A wrought iron stand for holding a consecrated lantern during the second ritual step.
- **Blood Consecration Basin**: A small silver basin where a drop of the ritualist's blood must be offered as the final step.
- **Sacred Inscriptions**: Ancient symbols and detailed ritual instructions carved into the stone around the altar's perimeter.

**DC Checks:**

- Religion DC 12: Understand the three-step ritual sequence for awakening the Ember Gourd.
- Arcana DC 13: Recognize the Ember Gourd's magical properties and how to properly channel its power.
- Investigation DC 12: Discover the detailed ritual instructions carved around the trough's rim.
- Performance DC 11: Perform the ritual with proper solemnity and precision.

**Plot Hooks:**

- The Ember Gourd pulses with inner fire when touched by someone with good intentions.
- The Altar Keeper's Shade appears when the ritual begins, providing cryptic guidance about the proper sequence.
- The three ritual stations around the altar must be activated in the correct order: grain, lantern, blood.
- Once awakened, the Ember Gourd becomes a powerful weapon against the Pumpkin King's magic.

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 15 | Disable DC 17 | Trigger DC 12 | Damage: 2d6 radiant (holy energy erupts from the altar if it's defiled or approached with evil intent)

**Loot:**

- The Ember Gourd (key artifact needed to complete the curse-breaking ritual)
- Ritual Chalice (silver cup worth 50 gp, required for certain blessing ceremonies)
- Sacred Candle Set (when lit together, creates a protective circle against undead for 1 hour)

**Encounters:**

- *ritual*: Three-step Ember Gourd awakening ritual requiring grain offering, lantern lighting, and blood consecration.
- *roleplay*: Interaction with the Altar Keeper's Shade to learn about the Ember Gourd's proper use.
- *combat*: Guardian Stone animates if the altar is threatened or desecrated.

**NPCs:**

- Altar Keeper's Shade (Mysterious and aloof; will provide guidance about the Ember Gourd's power if approached respectfully.)
- Sybil Nettlemire (Cryptic and playful; often answers questions with rhymes or snippets of song.)

**Monsters:**

- Guardian Stone -- The altar itself can animate partially if threatened, striking out with stone appendages to protect the sacred Ember Gourd.

*Connected to: Within area: V13, V15*

**DM Guidance:**

EMBER GOURD RITUAL: Requires 3-step sequence: 1) Grain offering (Religion DC 12), 2) Blessed lantern lighting (Performance DC 11), 3) Blood consecration (Constitution save DC 10, 1 HP sacrifice). Success on all three: Call updateCharacterInfo to add AWAKENED EMBER GOURD. Call updatePlot to mark ritual completion. Failure: Ritual must be restarted. On forcing altar: Trigger trap for 2d6 radiant damage.

### V15 -- Hayrick Larder

A storage area filled with towering stacks of hay and preserved grain, some reaching nearly to the ceiling. The air is thick with dust and the musty scent of old straw. Between the haystacks, narrow pathways wind like a maze, creating countless hiding spots and dead ends. Some of the hay bales show signs of disturbance, as if something large has been burrowing through them.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: Maze-like layout makes navigation difficult; some areas require climbing over or through hay bales.*

>A storage facility turned into a dangerous maze where the party must navigate both physical hazards and supernatural threats while uncovering more clues about the spreading curse.

**Features:**

- **Towering Haystacks**: Massive piles of hay and straw creating a complex three-dimensional maze.
- **Hidden Pathways**: Narrow corridors between hay bales that wind unpredictably through the storage area.
- **Disturbed Areas**: Sections where something large has burrowed or nested, leaving obvious signs of occupation.

**DC Checks:**

- Perception DC 13: Notice the carved symbols on hidden support beams.
- Survival DC 12: Navigate the maze of haystacks without getting lost.
- Investigation DC 14: Locate the hidden supply cache among the straw.

**Plot Hooks:**

- The Hay-Bound Spirit warns that something evil has been using the larder as a feeding ground.
- Deep within the hayricks, strange symbols have been carved into hidden support beams, matching those found at other cursed sites.

**Traps:**

## HLF001 -- The Hollow Field

The Hollow Field lies like a wound in the earth: a vast, circular clearing where nothing living dares take root, the soil stripped bare and scored with strange symbols and ritual rings. Around it, the land feels unnaturally hushed, yet the center never truly rests--there, a towering ring of corn stalks rises like a living wall, their leaves whispering in a voice that is not carried by any wind. The place feels less abandoned than watched. Every furrow, every sigil, every patch of blackened earth suggests that this emptiness is deliberate, a sacred absence maintained by older rites and hungrier powers than the villagers understand.

This area serves as a hinge in the adventure's larger mystery, tying together the dead memories of the Old Graveyard and the fervent dread of the Fields of Supplication. The barren circles and occult markings hint that the same force animates both the blighted harvest and the disturbed dead, and the central ring of stalks acts as a natural arena where that power gathers and manifests. The Hollow Field's encounters and chambers should feel like revelations: each one deepens the sense that the land itself has been enlisted in a ritual of appeasement or summoning, and that the party is moving closer to the heart of the curse. Paths from here lead back toward the graveyard's secrets or onward into the supplicant fields, making the Hollow Field both a destination and a crossroads in the investigation.

Within the stalk-ring dwell the Harvest Witnesses, grim observers or cult-bound figures who know more than they admit and may speak in hushed, superstitious fragments about what was offered here and why. They are shadowed by the true masters of the place: the Pumpkin King, an eerie sovereign of rot and harvest, and his Pumpkin Stalkers, which prowl between the stalks like living scarecrows given malice and hunger. Together they make the Hollow Field feel like a court of autumnal dread, where the land's silence is enforced by tooth, vine, and ritual. From this place, the adventure can branch naturally back to the Old Graveyard or deeper into the Fields of Supplication, each connection reinforcing that the same blight binds all three sites into one creeping harvest of doom.

**Area Type:** Mixed | **Recommended Level:** 3
