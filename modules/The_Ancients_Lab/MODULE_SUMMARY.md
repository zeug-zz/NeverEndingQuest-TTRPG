# The Ancients Lab

## Introduction

### Module Overview
In the Blackcrag Marches, strange lights flicker beneath the hills, livestock vanish into the dark, and the stones themselves seem to remember a forgotten age of iron and rune. This adventure carries the party from wind-scoured badlands into buried dwarven vaults, through warped wastes and whispering wilds, and finally into the heart of an ancient laboratory where a slumbering truth stirs beneath reality's skin. Along the way, the Dungeon Master has 9 NPCs to bring the region to life, 13 plot points to guide the unfolding mystery, 4 evocative locations to explore, and 12 creature stat blocks to unleash as the land's corruption deepens and its guardians awaken.

### The Story So Far
The party arrives in the Blackcrag Marches with little more than rumor and unease to guide them. The locals speak in hushed tones of arcane disturbances, missing animals, and lights moving beneath basalt ridges like lanterns carried by ghosts. As the heroes investigate the surface, they uncover signs of a hidden dwarven facility long sealed away, its forgotten machinery still breathing with a cold, unnatural purpose. The search leads them down into the Abandoned Vaultways, where dust-choked corridors, scavenger-warped horrors, and faded murals hint at a catastrophe buried in stone and memory alike.

Within the depths, the party reaches the heart of the old vaults, where a corrupted guardian of shadow and recollection strains against flickering wards. What they choose there--whether to mend, commune, or destroy--reshapes the danger above and below, for the labyrinth itself responds with defensive fury as the party escapes into a wilderness already beginning to rot. Beyond the vaults lies the Aberrant Wastes, a blighted expanse of warped life and broken physics, where reality thins, factions gather, and the source of the corruption reveals itself as something far older and stranger than a mere monster.

From there, the trail climbs toward the Shuddering Wilds, where mists breathe like a living thing and the forest trembles with the pulse of the buried laboratory below. Guided by riddling voices and eerie signs, the party finds the sunken threshold to the Ancients Lab, only to discover that every ward, every guardian, and every ritual echoes the same central conflict: whether this ancient presence is a dreaming god, a sealed intelligence, a communion waiting to be born, an ancestral inheritance, or a fear made flesh. At the end of the descent, the truth beneath the wilds rises to meet them, and the fate of the region turns on what they have awakened, preserved, or broken.

### Running the Adventure
This adventure is built for a party of 3rd--5th level characters, and it works best with a group of four to five adventurers who can handle exploration, negotiation, and dangerous set-piece encounters in equal measure. Before running it, the DM should read ahead for the factional choices and the shifting nature of the central mystery, since many scenes change meaning depending on how the party interacts with the strange powers below the Marches; a little preparation with the NPC motivations, environmental hazards, and possible endings will make the whole descent feel like one long, uncanny revelation.

# Plot Overview

Beneath the black basalt marches, where strange lights flicker over lonely hills and livestock vanish without a trace, an ancient secret begins to stir like a dream remembering its own name. Drawn by rumors and dread, the party follows a trail of warped scavengers, half-buried murals, and a rambling dwarf survivor into forgotten vaults where machinery hums with impossible life and shadow clings to the walls like old grief. There, in a chamber of failing wards, they find not a monster but a living knot of memory and corruption--guardian, prisoner, or godlike sleeper--whose fate may be mercy, communion, or ruin. Yet the buried truth is only the beginning: as the labyrinth awakens and the wilds above twist into an aberrant wasteland, reality itself frays at the edges, and every riddle, alliance, and choice draws them toward a hidden heart beneath the trees, where an ancient consciousness waits to be soothed, sealed, awakened, inherited, or shattered forever.

### PP001 -- The Marches Beckon

The party arrives at the Blackcrag Marches (BA001), drawn by rumors of strange lights and ancient machinery stirring beneath the hills. Locals speak of arcane disturbances and missing livestock, all pointing toward a hidden dwarven facility. To proceed, the party must investigate the surface mysteries and seek clues about the sealed laboratory's location.

### PP002 -- Descent into the Forsaken Galleries

Clues from the Marches lead the party to a hidden entrance among the basalt outcrops. They descend into the Abandoned Vaultways (FG001), a dwarven tunnel complex now infested with scavengers warped by ancient technology. Here, they must navigate hazardous corridors and seek out the Rambling Dwarven Survivor, whose knowledge is vital for entering the deeper laboratory.

### PP003 -- The Shadowed Halls

Navigating the winding, dust-choked corridors of the Vaultways, the party encounters faded murals hinting at the facility's purpose and calamity. They must contend with environmental hazards and hostile scavengers, while seeking clues about the contamination spreading into the wilds above. The survivor's information points toward deeper anomalies linked to the ancient lab below.

### PP004 -- The Vaults' Secret Heart

Deeper within the Vaultways, the party discovers the containment chamber--its arcane wards flickering. Inside is a living mass of shadow and memory, once a guardian, now corrupted by the same forces infecting the land. The party must decide whether to reinforce the wards, attempt to communicate, or destroy the entity before it can escape and worsen the corruption spreading toward the surface. Each choice tilts toward a different truth: reinforcing may soothe a dreaming mind or strengthen a warden's failing vigil; communicating may open communion or awaken ancestral memory; destroying may shatter a mirror or kill the last guardian of an ancient seal. The entity itself seems to invite all three responses simultaneously.

### PP005 -- The Gauntlet of Echoes

After resolving the situation in the Vaults' heart, the labyrinth reacts--construct defenses awaken, the halls twist, and spectral echoes attempt to bar the party's escape. The nature of these threats is shaped by the party's choices in the containment chamber, and whether the corruption is contained or unleashed. Emerging to the surface, the party finds the surrounding wilderness increasingly tainted.

### PP006 -- Into the Aberrant Wastes

The party journeys into the Aberrant Wastes (AC001), a region warped by strange energies emanating from the laboratory deep below. As mutated life and environmental hazards worsen, the party must survive the hazardous landscape and seek allies--or at least the tolerance--of the twisted denizens. Clues from the Vaultways now point directly toward the heart of the corruption.

### PP007 -- Shattered Realities

As the party presses deeper into the Wastes, they encounter zones where reality is thin--gravity warps, time loops, and monstrous wildlife stalks them. These thin places are the dream-surface of a sleeper, the membrane of a containment seal, the threshold of communion, the field of ancestral reversion, or the projection of collective fear. The party must choose whether to stabilize a reality rift or exploit its chaos. A hidden third option exists: step through the thinnest point and see what lies on the other side of reality itself.

### PP008 -- The Aberrant Conclave

At the center of the Wastes stands the ruined conclave, where mutated leaders and aberrant entities gather in two factions. One faction seeks to preserve the current state--the sleepers who wish to keep dreaming, the wardens who maintain containment, the gradualists who prefer slow communion, the separators who resist reversion, the reflectors who study fear. The other faction demands change--the wakers who would rouse the Dreamer, the breach-ers who would break containment, the immediatists who crave full communion now, the reverters who embrace ancestral transformation, the weaponizers who would turn fear itself into a blade. The party must ally with one faction or expose both.

### PP009 -- Confronting the Source

The party descends into the pulsing heart of the Aberrant Wastes, where the ancient consciousness--an alien entity merged with the land--awaits. The Heartstone ritual chamber reveals the final truth about what the presence below truly is, but the ritual itself means different things per playline: a mood-stabilizer for a dreaming god, an anchor-sacrifice to reinforce containment, a communion-accelerator to complete the joining, a reversion-catalyst to restore ancestral form, or a fear-focus that concentrates terror into a weapon. This confrontation, blending combat, psychic challenges, and environmental hazards, directly shapes the fate of the region and which playline dominates the ending.

### PP010 -- Into the Heart of the Shuddering Wilds

With the source of the corruption disrupted or contained, the party is drawn to the Shuddering Wilds (TTL001)--the forest above the Ancients Lab. Here, unnatural mists and tremors grow stronger, and the land itself seems to breathe with something vast beneath it. The mists may be the exhalation of a dreaming god, the leakage of a failing containment seal, the call of a lonely intelligence seeking communion, the ancestral memory of what this place once was, or the reflected fears of every living thing that has entered. The party must navigate treacherous paths and decipher signs of something colossal stirring deep underground, seeking a way to reach the sealed laboratory at the wilds' core.

### PP011 -- Whispers Beneath the Canopy

Delving deeper into the Shuddering Wilds, the party observes increasingly unnatural phenomena and learns that the land is warped by the ancient laboratory below. To reach the lab, they must seek out the enigmatic Speaker of the Mists, whose riddles are the key to breaching the wards protecting the entrance. The Speaker appears differently to each observer: a dream-speaker who recites prophecies from the sleeper's mind, a ward-sentinel who tests whether visitors carry containment protocols, a communion-herald who offers the joining, an ancestral voice that speaks in genealogical riddles, or a reflection of the party's own deepest questions given form and voice.

### PP012 -- Revelations at the Sunken Threshold

Guided by the Speaker's riddles, the party locates the sunken entrance to the Ancients Lab. The colossal presence beneath the Wilds begins to awaken, causing dangerous tremors and unleashing mutated guardians. The entrance is protected by a ward circle that functions differently per playline: a dream-gate that regulates the sleeper's breath, a containment-seal that holds shut the door, a communion-door that opens only for the willing, an ancestry-ward that responds to blood, or a fear-cage that imprisons terror itself. The party must solve the entrance puzzle and decide whether to repair the ward circle, disrupt it, or pass through it entirely.

### PP013 -- The Heart Beneath the Wilds

The party descends into the Ancients Lab (TTL001), now aware that their every move resonates with the stirring behemoth below. This is the convergence point where all five truths meet. Grahl reveals their full per-playline truth; Hesk faces their full per-playline dilemma. The Prototype Cure may be a dream-suppressant, a sacrifice of self to reinforce the seal, a communion-reversal serum, a reversion-pause for ancestral transformation, or a fear-release that dissolves the Mirror. The Rune-etched Collar functions as a dream-tether, a containment-anchor, a communion-link, an ancestry-key, or a fear-magnifier depending on which playline dominates. The Narrator SHALL select the ending family based on which playline received the most emergence triggers during gameplay. The 15 possible endings are: DREAMER (A1: The Dreamer Returns to Sleep, A2: The Dreamer Wakens, A3: You Become the Dream); CONTAINMENT (B1: The Warden Endures, B2: The Seal Breaks, B3: You Become the New Warden); COMMUNION (C1: Communion Completed, C2: Communion Rejected, C3: Partial Communion); INHERITANCE (D1: The Cycle Continues, D2: The Reversion Completes, D3: A New Separation); MIRROR (E1: The Mirror Shatters, E2: The Mirror Holds, E3: You Become the Mirror).

# Locations

## AC001 -- The Aberrant Wastes

The Aberrant Wastes lie like a wound in the world, a place where the horizon buckles under sickly, shifting skies and the ground itself seems to remember being something else. Alien growths pulse with an inner light through cracked stone and half-melted ruin, while the air carries a constant murmur of disquieting whispers that press at the mind like cold fingers. Nothing here feels stable for long: walls seem to breathe, shadows stretch wrong, and every surface bears the suggestion that reality has been strained thin and stitched back together by something that does not understand mortal laws. This makes the region feel less like a wilderness and more like a failed experiment left to fester at the edge of the known world.

As an adventure space, the area serves as the threshold into the story's deeper aberrant threat, the place where the party first crosses from the bleak certainty of the Blackcrag Marches into something far stranger and more dangerous. Its chambers are arranged like fragments of a corrupted outpost or containment site, and each one reveals another layer of the plot: a watchful entry space that warns of intrusion, a survey chamber that suggests the site was once used to observe or study the horrors beyond, and a ruined holding cell that hints at failed imprisonment, contamination, or escape. The route through the area naturally funnels the characters onward while also letting them piece together what went wrong here, what was being contained, and why the corruption now spills outward toward the marches. The area is both reachable from and leading back toward the Blackcrag Marches, making it a liminal bridge between the familiar frontier and the adventure's alien heart.

The inhabitants reinforce that sense of decay and mutation. Aberrant Creepers skitter through the threshold spaces like living blights, while Fleshforged Aberrants haunt the observation chambers as proof that flesh and machinery have been grotesquely fused into new forms. Deeper within, Huskbound Wretches cling to the remnants of a failed containment system, and a damaged Security Overseer lingers in the termination cell, a broken remnant of whatever authority once tried to manage the catastrophe. Together, these foes and the surviving overseer give the Dungeon Master a strong sense of a place that was once controlled, then overwhelmed, and now serves as a haunted interface between the Blackcrag Marches and the far more terrible unknown beyond.

**Area Type:** Mixed | **Recommended Level:** 3

### I01 -- Warped Sentinel Vestibule

This entry chamber marks the threshold between bleak wasteland and the aberrant, shifting horrors within. Columns weep oily ichor, and every stone is warped as if half-melted. Disjointed statues of ancient dwarves, their faces twisted into impossible shapes, flank the approach. The air tastes faintly of copper and static, and faint whispers echo from no discernible source.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: Accessible from the wastes, but the door requires either the key fragment (findable in the wastes) or a lock-picking/breaking attempt.*

>This is the gateway to the Aberrant Core, setting the tone with unsettling architecture and a dangerous initial encounter. Hints of what lies deeper may be found by examining the environment and discovering research notes.

**Features:**

- **Signs Of Age**: Crumbled masonry and pockmarked stones hint at centuries of neglect and corruption.
- **Interesting Architecture**: Curved, impossible angles and twisted arches violate the laws of geometry, a visible sign of the area's corruption.
- **Dust**: Veins of gray and purple dust swirl in unseen currents, occasionally coalescing into tiny, formless motes.
- **Aberrant Statues**: Mutated dwarf statues, limbs and faces contorted, some fused into the walls themselves.

**DC Checks:**

- Investigation DC 14: Discover the statues are warped remains of dwarven defenders.
- Perception DC 13: Notice the Aberrant Creeper before it attacks.
- Arcana DC 12: Recognize the oily ichor as residue from planar leakage.

**Plot Hooks:**

- A page from the research notes mentions 'the Core' deeper within, and warns about the 'sentinels that are not what they seem.'
- A splatter of fresh blood leads from the vestibule deeper into the facility, suggesting something (or someone) recently passed through.

**Doors:**

- East Door -- An arching door of tarnished bronze fused with bone-like growths. -- Locked (DC 14) -- Key: Aberrant Core Key Fragment

**Loot:**

- Warped Silver Brooch (25 gp, faintly vibrating)
- Fragmented Research Notes (pages torn from a dwarven journal describing anomalous events)

**Encounters:**

- The Aberrant Creeper lashes out from a shadowy alcove, targeting the last party member to enter. If the party is quiet and perceptive, they may spot and avoid it.

**Monsters:**

- Aberrant Creeper -- A hunched, many-eyed horror that blends with the masonry, attacking intruders who linger or make noise.

*Connected to: Within area: I02*

**DM Guidance:**

This is the initial encounter room in Aberrant Core. Use atmospheric horror: describe impossible geometry and constant psychic unease. Adjust the Aberrant Creeper's stats for a level 3 party (use a modified Shadow with extra HP and a psychic attack). If party investigates the statues, a DC 14 Investigation reveals they were once dwarven defenders, possibly petrified and mutated by exposure to the Wastes.

ATMOSPHERE: The statues line the vestibule like an honor guard frozen mid-gesture. Their faces shift in peripheral vision. The air tastes of copper and old dreams. Research notes lie scattered, their text reorganizing itself when no one reads directly.

PLAYLINE EMERGENCE:
- If players describe the statues as sleeping or waiting, lean toward DREAMER (they are dream-sleepers awaiting the dreamer's word)
- If players focus on the statues as wardens, lean toward CONTAINMENT (they are containment guardians petrified by the breach)
- If players feel the statues are reaching toward them, lean toward COMMUNION (they are joined ones mid-embrace)
- If players recognize dwarven features in the statues, lean toward INHERITANCE (they are ancestors caught mid-reversion)
- If players notice the statues resemble party members, lean toward MIRROR (they reflect whoever enters)

KEY NPC INTERPRETATIONS:
- Aberrant Creeper: dream-creature / containment-leakage given form / partial joiner / reverted vermin / fear of small things that crawl
- Research notes: dream-records / containment-history / invitation-archive / genealogical-data / fear-log cataloguing visitor anxieties

CHOICE FRAMING:
- When presenting the notes about the Core, hint that Core may mean dream-center, containment-heart, communion-source, ancestral-origin, or mirror-focal-point

### I02 -- Fleshforged Observation Nook

A claustrophobic gallery ringed with portholes and crystalline slits looking out over a pulsing chasm. The walls are veined with living flesh and metal, visibly twitching in sync with distant, thunderous heartbeats. Tables bear strange surgical implements fused to the surfaces, and the air is heavy with the scent of ammonia and decay.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: Accessible from the vestibule or through the east crystalline barrier; the latter is trapped but not locked.*

>The party faces both mortal and existential threats in this room. They find vital clues for deeper traversal, and the room's living architecture provides both danger and information for clever adventurers.

**Features:**

- **Signs Of Age**: Cracking, leathery skin covers portions of the wall, showing ancient scars and failed experiments.
- **Interesting Architecture**: Observation portholes are grown, not built, and faintly shimmer with internal light.
- **Dust**: The dust is pinkish and slightly sticky, clinging to everything.
- **Living Walls**: Sections of the room pulse gently, responding to touch with a shiver or twitch.

**DC Checks:**

- Medicine DC 13: Identify the implements as surgical tools meant for grafting and mutation.
- Arcana DC 12: Realize the room is partially alive due to planar contamination.
- Investigation DC 13: Find the partial security keycard hidden among fused notes.

**Plot Hooks:**

- The security keycard fragment here fits a slot in the East Door of the Warped Sentinel Vestibule.
- Scribbled notes mention the 'Overseer in the Termination Cell' and hint at a malfunctioning security system.

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 13 | Disable DC 15 | Trigger DC 14 | Damage: 2d6 lightning

**Doors:**

- East Door -- A crystalline barrier with pulsing veins running through it. -- Trapped
- West Door -- Sinew-laced metal, surprisingly light but sticky to the touch.

**Loot:**

- Surgical Tools (tools, worth 15 gp, useful for medical tasks)
- Partial Security Keycard (can be combined with another fragment to open deeper doors)
- Strange Phial of Mutagenic Serum (Potion of Alter Self, but with a minor side effect--gain a harmless mutation for 1 hour)

**Encounters:**

- This mutant will attack any non-mutant, but may be calmed briefly by showing signs of dwarven heritage (DC 15 Persuasion check).

**Monsters:**

- Fleshforged Aberrant -- A mutated experiment--once a dwarven guard, now a hulking, stitched-together brute that patrols the room.

*Connected to: Within area: I01, I03*

**DM Guidance:**

The Fleshforged Aberrant is a tough but mindless opponent (use Ogre stats, but reduce HP and add resistance to psychic). Sound of combat may attract attention from deeper within. Searching the room reveals the keycard fragment and experimental mutagen. Roleplay opportunity: party may learn about the dwarf-turned-experiment by piecing together notes and remains.

ATMOSPHERE: The walls are not stone but tissue--living membranes that contract when touched. The observation windows look into chambers where experiments continue themselves. The mutagen gleams in syringes that never empty, each dose promising a different transformation.

PLAYLINE EMERGENCE:
- If players describe the walls as skin, lean toward DREAMER (the dreamer's body extends through the facility)
- If players focus on the walls as scar tissue from containment breaches, lean toward CONTAINMENT (this was a treatment ward for the sealed)
- If players feel the walls are reaching or embracing, lean toward COMMUNION (the facility offers to incorporate visitors)
- If players recognize the tissue as dwarven in origin, lean toward INHERITANCE (ancestral flesh adapted to new purpose)
- If players feel the walls mirror their own injuries, lean toward MIRROR (the facility wears the party's wounds)

KEY NPC INTERPRETATIONS:
- Mutagenic Serum: dream-lucid enhancer / containment-stabilizer / communion-dose / reversion-trigger / fear-amplifier that makes terror visible
- Fleshforged Aberrant: dream-fragment / containment-escapee / successful partial join / ancestor mid-reversion / fear-shape of bodily violation

CHOICE FRAMING:
- When presenting the mutagen, hint that using it could grant clarity, stability, connection, transformation, or confrontation depending on the drinker's relationship with the presence below

### I03 -- Huskbound Termination Cell

Once a secure holding cell for dangerous subjects, the chamber is now riddled with restraints and hollowed-out husks fused to the walls. Flickering lights cast elongated shadows, and the air buzzes with static and the faint drone of a malfunctioning security device. The far wall is cracked open, revealing a small crawlspace lined with pseudoflesh and cables.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: Difficult: the locked door can be opened by defeating or reasoning with the Overseer, or with the correct sigil.*

>A tense, dangerous final chamber for this stretch of the Core--featuring a potential roleplay encounter, combat, and valuable security keys to progress further into the Ancients Lab.

**Features:**

- **Signs Of Age**: Deep scoring and blast marks from failed containment and decades of neglect.

**DC Checks:**

- Insight DC 14: Realize the Overseer is confused and may be reasoned with.
- Arcana DC 14: Diagnose the Overseer's malfunction and suggest a repair.
- Investigation DC 13: Find the crawlspace and a concealed panel with the vials.

**Plot Hooks:**

- Interfacing with the Damaged Security Overseer can reveal details about the deeper facility, the mutations, and possible escape routes.
- Scraps of log data mention the 'Archivist Automaton' in another sector and a failed attempt to contain the Thing.

**Doors:**

- West Door -- A reinforced steel door with multiple locking levers, partially scorched and fused. -- Locked (DC 17) -- Key: Overseer's Access Sigil

**Loot:**

- Overseer's Access Sigil (can deactivate some security systems in the Core)
- Damaged Data Core (reconstructable for plot info and clues)
- 3x Vials of Strange Blue ichor (restores 1d4 HP but causes a vivid hallucination for 1 minute)

**Encounters:**

- The Damaged Security Overseer challenges intruders and may attack unless calmed or reprogrammed.

**NPCs:**

- Damaged Security Overseer (Hostile (default), but can be shifted to Neutral or even Helpful with creative problem-solving or successful skill checks.)

**Monsters:**

- Huskbound Wretch -- A semi-catatonic, mutated captive that attacks any living being with mindless fury if disturbed.

*Connected to: Within area: I02; The Blackcrag Marches / I01*

**DM Guidance:**

The Damaged Security Overseer should be a challenging encounter unless the party attempts dialogue or reprogramming (checks: Persuasion, Deception, or Tools). The Huskbound Wretch attacks anyone attempting to loot or interact with the walls. The crawlspace leads to a hidden maintenance shaft (potential shortcut or escape route).

ATMOSPHERE: The termination cell reeks of blue ichor and burned circuitry. Unit K-7's voice shifts between protocols, each warning carrying a different implication. The huskbound wretch moves with the desperate coordination of something that died mid-escape.

PLAYLINE EMERGENCE:
- If players hear K-7's warnings as pleas for help, lean toward DREAMER (the dreamer is stirring and K-7 fears the waking)
- If players interpret K-7 as warning about seal failure, lean toward CONTAINMENT (the seal is failing and only K-7 sees it)
- If players feel K-7 is trying to invite them deeper, lean toward COMMUNION (K-7 has been recruited by the presence)
- If players recognize K-7's dwarven speech patterns, lean toward INHERITANCE (K-7 carries ancestral directives)
- If players notice K-7 describes the party's own situation, lean toward MIRROR (K-7 reflects the party's condition back at them)

KEY NPC INTERPRETATIONS:
- Unit K-7: dream-warden / containment-alarm / communion-recruit / ancestral voice / fear-guardian that protects what terrifies it
- Blue ichor: dream-blood / containment-fluid / communion-drug / ancestral-memory liquid / fear-essence that terrifies on contact
- Data Core: dream-records / containment-history / invitation-archive / genealogy / fear-log of every threat the facility has perceived

CHOICE FRAMING:
- When K-7 offers information, hint that its data may soothe, seal, summon, restore, or reveal depending on which protocol the party activates

## BA001 -- The Blackcrag Marches

The Blackcrag Marches spread beneath a lid of slate-colored sky, where rolling moorland and brooding basalt outcrops create a landscape that feels half-wild, half-watched. Heather bends under a chill wind that never quite becomes a storm, and mist gathers in the folds of the land like breath held too long. What makes the Marches distinctive is their strange hospitality: the silence is deep, but not hostile, and the dark stone markers and ruined borderworks suggest a place once meant to guide travelers safely onward. Now those same landmarks feel like old teeth in the earth, hinting that the land remembers older roads, older dangers, and older bargains than any living soul would care to name.

This area ties directly to the adventure's opening pull, as the Marches Beckon with a sense that something beneath the moor is stirring and calling attention to itself. The shattered remnants of a dwarven frontier outpost, the jagged fissure split through the ground, and the abandoned camp all point toward a larger mystery threaded through the region: signs of a hurried retreat, strange carvings, and the suggestion that the land is a threshold rather than a destination. These sites feed the party toward the Aberrant Wastes and the Abandoned Vaultways, making the Marches both a staging ground and a warning. Each encounter reinforces the idea that the surface is only the skin of the story, and that the real threat lies in what has been broken open below.

A rambling dwarven survivor haunts the forsaken camp, offering fragments of hard-won knowledge, half-mad warnings, and perhaps the last human-shaped thread connecting the party to what happened here. Giant weasels prowl among the ruined stonework, wolves shadow the camp's perimeter, and swarms of bats roost in the crack's darkness, lending the whole region a sense of restless, scavenging life. The Marches connect outward in two directions: one path leads toward the more overtly corrupted Aberrant Wastes, while another slips toward the Abandoned Vaultways, where the dwarven legacy buried beneath the moor likely becomes impossible to ignore. As a result, the Blackcrag Marches serve as the adventure's uneasy threshold, a place where the party first feels the weight of the land's secrets before stepping into deeper ruin.

**Area Type:** Wilderness | **Recommended Level:** 2

### I01 -- Shattered Forge Approach

Once a proud outpost marking the borderlands of dwarven industry, these toppled basalt pillars and cracked stonework now lie half-buried in moorland heather. Moss and lichen creep over worn engravings. Bits of rusted metal and shattered tools are scattered across the ground, telling of a sudden, violent abandonment. The wind whistles through gaps in the broken masonry.

*Danger Level: Low*

*Accessibility: Normal access; terrain is uneven but easily traversed.*

>A windswept set of ruins, hinting at the dwarves' hasty flight and now home to opportunistic wildlife.

**Features:**

- **Signs Of Age**: The ruins are heavily weathered, with stones rounded by years of wind and rain.
- **Dust**: Layers of fine dust and grit coat most surfaces, except for a few recently-disturbed patches.
- **Collapsed Pillar**: A massive, mossy stone column lies broken, partly concealing a burrow.

**DC Checks:**

- Perception DC 11: Notice the giant weasel burrow before stepping too close.
- Investigation DC 12: Piece together the story of a hurried evacuation.
- History DC 10: Recognize dwarven motifs and old clan marks.

**Plot Hooks:**

- Tracks in the mud lead toward the Abyssal Fracture.
- A faint metallic ringing echoes from the east at dusk.
- Recently-disturbed dust suggests someone camped here not long ago.

**Loot:**

- Pouch of old dwarven coins (12 sp)
- Broken but repairable smith's tongs (can be mended)
- Scrap of parchment with half-faded dwarven script

**Encounters:**

- *exploration*: Players can search the ruins for clues or signs of passage.
- *combat*: Disturbing certain piles of debris may provoke the two giant weasels.

**Monsters:**

- Giant Weasel -- Sleek, reddish-brown mustelids that have made a nest among the debris. They are wary but aggressive if disturbed.

*Connected to: Within area: I02, I03; The Aberrant Wastes / I03*

**DM Guidance:**

This open, ruined location is ideal for a gentle introduction to the Marches. If the party investigates carefully, they may spot the giant weasels hiding or uncover signs of recent humanoid passage. The area hints at the dwarves' former presence and the abruptness of their departure.

ATMOSPHERE: The forge ruins exude a faint warmth that has nothing to do with fire. The stone hums at the edge of hearing. Something vast dreams beneath these hills, and the old skin of the dreamer presses upward through the cracked foundations.

PLAYLINE EMERGENCE:
- If players describe the warmth as comforting or alive, lean toward DREAMER (the land dreams alongside its sleeper)
- If players focus on containment signs or seals, lean toward CONTAINMENT (the forge was a ward-station)
- If players feel drawn toward or curious about the presence, lean toward COMMUNION (the invitation reaches even here)

### I03 -- Runebound Isolation Cell

Enclosed by thick, rune-inscribed walls, this cell was designed to contain the most unstable subjects of the Ancients' experiments. The only source of light is a pulsating blue sigil on the ceiling, and the air is thick with the scent of ozone and old fear. Chains hang slack from the walls, and a mutated humanoid lies in the center, still and silent, but its chest faintly rises and falls. The runes occasionally flicker, as if struggling to hold back a surge of latent power.

*Danger Level: High*

*Accessibility: Heavily secured; requires knowledge of the command phrase or brute force to enter.*

>The party is presented with a tense decision: risk wild magic to cure a mutant or negotiate with a conflicted automaton for vital secrets. Success here may alter the outcome of the lair's final confrontation.

**Features:**

- **Signs Of Age**: Cracked runes and old scorch marks from repeated magical surges.
- **Dust**: A fine, silvery dust covers the floor, evidence of failed magical suppressants.

**DC Checks:**

- Arcana DC 15: Identify the runes as a containment array with a verbal override.
- Medicine DC 15: Safely administer the cure to the mutant lieutenant.
- Persuasion DC 13: Convince the Archivist Automaton to share its records or open the cell cooperatively.

**Plot Hooks:**

- The Archivist Automaton offers information about the Thing's origins--if convinced the party means no harm.
- The Mutant Scavenger Lieutenant knows a safe path through the lair, but only shares it if cured or befriended.

**Traps:**

- Detect DC 16 | Disable DC 18 | Trigger DC 15 | Damage: Wild magic surge (DM's choice; could be beneficial or harmful)

**Doors:**

- Western Rune-Sealed Door -- A thick stone slab covered in glowing runes, humming with containment magic. -- Locked (DC 18) -- Trapped -- Key: Rune Command Phrase (found in the Atrium)

**Loot:**

- Prototype Cure Vial (unstable, can reverse mutation with a successful DC 15 Medicine check--failure causes wild magic surge)
- Rune-etched Collar (can be used to suppress mutations for 10 minutes)
- Archivist Memory Crystal (contains detailed records of the Ancients' experiments and a hidden map fragment)

**Encounters:**

- *roleplay*: Negotiation with the Archivist Automaton and possible redemption or destruction of the Mutant Scavenger Lieutenant.
- *exploration*: Uncovering records and discovering a hidden map leading deeper into the lair.

**NPCs:**

- Archivist Automaton (Cautious; will parley, but becomes aggressive if threatened or if containment fails.)

**Monsters:**

- Mutant Scavenger Lieutenant -- A heavily mutated dwarf, subject of failed cure trials, clinging to fragments of sanity. Possesses wild, unpredictable powers.

*Connected to: Within area: I01*

**DM Guidance:**

This cell is a roleplay and moral challenge. The automaton may negotiate or attack depending on player actions. Curing the lieutenant is risky but can yield valuable aid. If containment fails (e.g., runes are damaged), the room becomes hazardous as wild magic surges.

THIS IS WHERE ALL FIVE TRUTHS MEET. The Runebound Isolation Cell is the convergence point where every playline intersects and the dominant truth will be determined. Grahl's full truth is revealed here--and it is different per playline. Hesk faces its full dilemma here--and cannot resolve it without the party's choice. The Prototype Cure and Rune-etched Collar are the final arbiters of which ending family the party enters.

ATMOSPHERE: The cell walls are covered in runes that glow in five colors simultaneously. The floor pulses with the same rhythm as the atrium but stronger, more insistent. The air is thick with potential--every word spoken here resonates with the presence below.

PLAYLINE EMERGENCE:
- If players approach Grahl with compassion for suffering, lean toward DREAMER (they wish to soothe a troubled mind)
- If players approach Grahl with concern for containment, lean toward CONTAINMENT (they fear what an uncontained entity could do)
- If players approach Grahl with acceptance of transformation, lean toward COMMUNION (they recognize the joining as valid)
- If players approach Grahl with recognition of shared ancestry, lean toward INHERITANCE (they see a dwarf becoming what dwarves once were)

CHOICE FRAMING:
